Psychiatry for Residents · Communication skills

Improving communication skills.

International medical graduates often find the practical side of day-to-day communication the hardest part of starting in the NHS: the phone call to a worried relative, the handover, explaining a section, the 01:00 bleep. Good communication starts on day one of your posting, and practising it on the job, every day, is the best preparation for the CASC. Every challenge is a real conversation from the ward, the clinic or the 01:00 bleep: the exact words, the questions to ask, how to explain, how to handle a worried relative, and the note you write.
