Aim and objectives

Aim. To build rapid recognition of autism, ADHD, other neurodevelopmental disorders and tic disorders so you can answer MRCPsych Paper B extended matching items accurately.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. State the prevalence of autism (about 1 to 2%) and ADHD (about 5%), their sex ratios, and autism's heritability of about 80%.
  2. Apply ICD-11 ADHD criteria, including onset before 12 and impairment in more than one setting, and distinguish ADHD from autism.
  3. Apply NICE NG87 drug order for children: methylphenidate first, then lisdexamfetamine, then atomoxetine or guanfacine, monitoring growth, pulse and blood pressure.
  4. Define Tourette syndrome as multiple motor and at least one vocal tic for over one year, and state its prevalence of about 1%.
  5. Apply explanation and behavioural therapy (CBIT or habit reversal) before drugs such as aripiprazole or clonidine for tics.
  6. Describe the course of tics: onset at five to seven, peak at ten to twelve, and fading through the teens.
  7. Answer the chapter's 169 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
