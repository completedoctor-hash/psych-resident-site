Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to recognise, assess the physical risk of, and treat eating disorders in children and young people for MRCPsych Paper B.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Compare the age of onset and sex ratio of anorexia nervosa, bulimia nervosa, binge eating disorder and ARFID.
  2. Explain how an anxious, perfectionist temperament interacts with dieting, puberty and weight pressure, and why families do not cause eating disorders.
  3. Assess physical risk using percentage median BMI and the MEED guidance, recognising bradycardia, hypothermia and electrolyte disturbance.
  4. Distinguish ARFID from anorexia nervosa by the motive for restricting food.
  5. Apply family therapy for anorexia nervosa as first-line treatment under 18, and outline refeeding syndrome risk, including hypophosphataemia.
  6. Describe outcomes, including recovery in most young people and anorexia nervosa's highest mortality in psychiatry.
  7. Answer the chapter's 65 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
