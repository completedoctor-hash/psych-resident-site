Aim and objectives

Aim. To explain how mental health services deliver coordinated care and how prevention is classified, for MRCPsych Paper B.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Match a patient's age, diagnosis, urgency and engagement to the right team, such as CMHT, crisis resolution or early intervention in psychosis.
  2. Describe the features of effective multidisciplinary teams, including a shared goal and psychological safety.
  3. Outline the psychiatrist's role in care planning and coordination, including legal duties and transitions between services.
  4. Describe medical, psychological and social interventions, including Individual Placement and Support for employment.
  5. Distinguish primary, secondary and tertiary prevention from Gordon's universal, selective and indicated prevention, with one example of each.
  6. Answer the chapter's 58 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
