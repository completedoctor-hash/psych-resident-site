Aim and objectives

Aim. To build rapid recognition of psychiatric presentations and safe prescribing in physical illness so you can answer MRCPsych Paper B extended matching items accurately.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Recognise physical illnesses presenting as psychiatric disorder, such as hypothyroidism, Cushing's syndrome and pancreatic cancer presenting with depression.
  2. Choose psychotropics safely in hepatic, renal and cardiac disease, such as avoiding lithium in renal failure and QTc-prolonging drugs in heart disease.
  3. Relate psychiatric sequelae of stroke, head injury and epilepsy to the site of the lesion, such as disinhibition after orbitofrontal injury.
  4. Recognise neuropsychiatric presentations of Huntington's disease, Wilson's disease, multiple sclerosis, HIV and anti-NMDA receptor encephalitis.
  5. Diagnose functional neurological symptoms on positive signs, such as Hoover's sign.
  6. Distinguish normal grief from prolonged grief disorder and depression at the end of life.
  7. Answer the chapter's 26 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
