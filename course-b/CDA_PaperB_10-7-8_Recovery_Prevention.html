Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to apply motivational, relapse-prevention, harm-reduction and population prevention approaches to substance misuse for MRCPsych Paper B.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Describe Prochaska and DiClemente's stages of change and the OARS skills of motivational interviewing.
  2. Explain Marlatt and Gordon's relapse prevention model, including high-risk situations and the abstinence violation effect.
  3. Outline harm-reduction measures, including needle and syringe programmes, take-home naloxone and opioid substitution treatment.
  4. Describe the roles of carers, mutual aid groups (e.g. Alcoholics Anonymous, SMART Recovery) and residential rehabilitation in recovery.
  5. Explain population prevention, such as minimum unit pricing, and apply AUDIT-C screening with a brief intervention.
  6. Answer the chapter's 40 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
