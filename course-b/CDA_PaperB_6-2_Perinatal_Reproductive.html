Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to assess and manage mental illness in the perinatal period and across the reproductive cycle for MRCPsych Paper B.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Describe the timing of perinatal illness and identify women at highest risk of postnatal relapse, such as those with bipolar disorder or previous postpartum psychosis.
  2. Adapt risk assessment to include neglect, harm to the baby and suicide risk across the first postnatal year.
  3. Outline same-day management of postpartum psychosis, including admission to a mother and baby unit.
  4. Compare the safety of lithium, valproate, SSRIs and antipsychotics in pregnancy and breastfeeding, and explain why valproate is avoided.
  5. Describe assessment of the parent-infant relationship and interventions such as video interaction guidance.
  6. Distinguish premenstrual dysphoric disorder from premenstrual exacerbation using a prospective symptom diary over at least two cycles.
  7. Answer the chapter's 131 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
