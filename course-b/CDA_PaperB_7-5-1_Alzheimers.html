Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to explain the epidemiology, pathology, presentation, treatment and course of Alzheimer's disease for MRCPsych Paper B.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. State that Alzheimer's disease is the commonest dementia and that its prevalence roughly doubles every five years after 65.
  2. Describe amyloid plaques and tau tangles, and name genetic risks: APP, PSEN1, PSEN2 and APOE e4.
  3. Distinguish typical amnestic Alzheimer's disease from atypical forms such as posterior cortical atrophy and logopenic aphasia.
  4. Outline NICE drug treatment (acetylcholinesterase inhibitors, memantine) and non-drug care for distress, carers and advance planning.
  5. Describe the course of Alzheimer's disease and list factors affecting progression, such as age at onset, behaviour, delirium and carer strain.
  6. Answer the chapter's 99 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
