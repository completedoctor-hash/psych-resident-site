Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to explain the indications, practice and outcomes of ECT, rTMS, DBS and psychosurgery for MRCPsych Paper B.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. List the main indications for ECT, including severe depression, catatonia and severe mania, and describe its adverse effects, including memory impairment.
  2. Compare rTMS, deep brain stimulation and ablative neurosurgery by indication, invasiveness and evidence.
  3. Describe the legal routes for giving ECT, including valid consent and sections 58A and 62 of the Mental Health Act 1983.
  4. Explain seizure threshold titration and how dose differs between bilateral and right unilateral electrode placement.
  5. Outline the pre-ECT anaesthetic workup and the roles of the induction agent and the muscle relaxant suxamethonium.
  6. Answer the chapter's 55 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
