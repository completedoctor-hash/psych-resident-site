Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare candidates to answer MRCPsych Paper B questions on the epidemiology, causes, diagnosis, treatment and outcome of schizophrenia.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. State the lifetime risk of schizophrenia (about 1%) and describe variation by sex, urban birth, migration and ethnicity.
  2. Describe genetic and environmental risk factors, including cannabis, and the dopamine hypothesis of psychosis.
  3. Apply ICD-11 criteria for schizophrenia, including one-month duration, and distinguish it from schizoaffective, delusional and acute transient psychotic disorders.
  4. Outline NICE treatment: antipsychotic, CBT for psychosis and family intervention, with clozapine after two adequate antipsychotic trials.
  5. Describe clozapine monitoring, including full blood counts for neutropenia and checks for myocarditis and constipation.
  6. List good and poor prognostic factors, including duration of untreated psychosis and high expressed emotion.
  7. Answer the chapter's 127 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
