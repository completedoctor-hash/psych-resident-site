Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare candidates to answer MRCPsych Paper B questions on the epidemiology, causes, presentation, treatment and course of frontotemporal dementia.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. State that FTD causes about 2% of dementia and is a leading cause of dementia between ages 45 and 65.
  2. Name the three main proteins (tau, TDP-43, FUS) and the common causative genes MAPT, GRN and C9orf72.
  3. Distinguish behavioural variant FTD from semantic and non-fluent variant primary progressive aphasia by presenting features.
  4. Recognise disinhibition, apathy, loss of empathy, hyperorality and compulsive behaviour as early bvFTD features in a vignette.
  5. Outline management: no disease-modifying drug, SSRIs or trazodone for behaviour, avoiding cholinesterase inhibitors, and support for carers.
  6. Describe the usual course of about 6 to 11 years, shorter with motor neurone disease and longer in semantic variant.
  7. Answer the chapter's 65 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
