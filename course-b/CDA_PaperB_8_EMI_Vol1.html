Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare candidates for MRCPsych Paper B EMI questions on psychodynamic, cognitive behavioural and family and systemic therapies.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Distinguish key ideas of Freud, Klein, Winnicott, Bion and Jung, such as the paranoid-schizoid position, good-enough mother and containment.
  2. Recognise defence mechanisms, transference and countertransference in a clinical vignette.
  3. List selection criteria for exploratory psychodynamic therapy, including psychological mindedness, motivation and ego strength.
  4. Match DIT, MBT, TFP and CAT to their signature tools, such as mentalising in MBT and the reformulation letter in CAT.
  5. Describe third-wave therapies, EMDR, trauma-focused CBT and the components of DBT.
  6. Distinguish structural, strategic and Milan family therapy by technique, and describe family intervention for psychosis.
  7. Answer the chapter's 148 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
