Aim and objectives

Aim. To build rapid recognition of the epidemiology, causes, presentation, treatment and course of the dementias and delirium so you can answer MRCPsych Paper B extended matching items accurately.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Describe Alzheimer's pathology (amyloid plaques and tau tangles), APOE e4 risk, and prevalence roughly doubling every five years after 65.
  2. Apply NICE guidance on donepezil, rivastigmine, galantamine and memantine by severity of Alzheimer's disease.
  3. Recognise vascular dementia from stepwise decline, focal signs and executive slowing, and name hypertension as its main modifiable risk factor.
  4. Apply the four core features of DLB and explain severe antipsychotic sensitivity in Lewy body disease.
  5. Distinguish behavioural-variant FTD from semantic and non-fluent primary progressive aphasia, and name tau, TDP-43 and FUS as causal proteins.
  6. Distinguish delirium, mild cognitive impairment and amnestic syndrome, and apply the 4AT and NICE delirium prevention measures.
  7. Answer the chapter's 295 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
