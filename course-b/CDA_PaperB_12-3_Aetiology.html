Aim and objectives

Aim. To explain the biological and psychosocial causes of intellectual disability for MRCPsych Paper B.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Classify biological causes of intellectual disability as prenatal, perinatal or postnatal, giving two examples of each.
  2. Name the genetic basis of Down, fragile X, Prader-Willi, Angelman, Williams and Rett syndromes.
  3. Recognise behavioural phenotypes, such as hyperphagia in Prader-Willi and hand-wringing in Rett syndrome, in a clinical vignette.
  4. Describe how family, social and educational environments influence intellectual and emotional development.
  5. Define secondary handicap and give two examples of how it arises.
  6. Answer the chapter's 25 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
