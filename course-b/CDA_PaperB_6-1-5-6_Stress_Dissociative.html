Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to recognise and manage stress-associated and dissociative disorders, from PTSD to functional neurological disorder, for MRCPsych Paper B.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Describe the epidemiology of PTSD, including higher risk in women and after interpersonal trauma, and predictors such as poor social support.
  2. Distinguish ICD-11 acute stress reaction, adjustment disorder, PTSD and complex PTSD by core features and time course.
  3. Outline NICE treatment of PTSD: trauma-focused CBT or EMDR first, medication second, and no psychological debriefing.
  4. Explain predisposing, precipitating and perpetuating factors in dissociative and functional disorders, including predictive processing accounts.
  5. Recognise positive signs of functional neurological disorder, such as Hoover's sign, and list the ICD-11 dissociative disorders.
  6. Outline treatment of dissociative disorders, from clear explanation to physiotherapy and CBT, and list predictors of a good outcome.
  7. Answer the chapter's 76 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
