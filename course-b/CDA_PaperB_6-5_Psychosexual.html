Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to assess sexual dysfunction and gender incongruence for MRCPsych Paper B.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Describe the phases of the sexual response cycle and classify sexual dysfunctions by the phase affected.
  2. Identify physical, psychological, relationship and medication causes, such as SSRIs and antipsychotic-induced hyperprolactinaemia.
  3. Outline treatments, including sensate focus and phosphodiesterase-5 inhibitors such as sildenafil.
  4. Define gender incongruence and explain its ICD-11 placement in the chapter on conditions related to sexual health.
  5. Distinguish ICD-11 gender incongruence from DSM-5 gender dysphoria, and outline a multidisciplinary assessment.
  6. Answer the chapter's 35 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
