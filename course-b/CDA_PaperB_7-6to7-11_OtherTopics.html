Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to assess and manage suicide risk, personality, therapy, bereavement, sleep and sexual health in older people for MRCPsych Paper B.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Explain why self-harm in older people carries high suicidal intent, and list risk factors such as depression, pain, isolation and male sex.
  2. Describe how personality disorder changes with age and why new personality change after about 60 prompts a search for organic causes.
  3. Outline how psychotherapy is adapted for older people and recognise reversed transference and countertransference.
  4. Distinguish normal grief from ICD-11 prolonged grief disorder (at least six months after loss) and adjustment disorder.
  5. Describe age-related sleep changes and explain why CBT for insomnia comes before hypnotics.
  6. Outline assessment of sexual problems in older people, including capacity to consent to sex in care settings.
  7. Answer the chapter's 59 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
