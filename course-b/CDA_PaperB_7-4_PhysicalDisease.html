Aim and objectives

Aim. To explain the psychiatric aspects of neurological, cerebrovascular and chronic physical illness in older people for MRCPsych Paper B.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Describe psychiatric features of Parkinson's disease, Huntington's disease and Parkinson-plus disorders, including impulse control disorders on dopamine agonists.
  2. Outline management of psychosis in Parkinson's disease, avoiding typical antipsychotics and using quetiapine or clozapine.
  3. Describe post-stroke depression and the psychiatric effects of sensory loss, including Charles Bonnet syndrome.
  4. Explain how pain, disability and loss of role can turn a normal reaction to illness into depression or anxiety.
  5. List reversible causes of cognitive impairment, such as hypothyroidism, B12 deficiency and normal pressure hydrocephalus, and the tests that find them.
  6. Answer the chapter's 68 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
