Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to recognise, assess risk in and manage eating disorders for MRCPsych Paper B.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Compare the prevalence of binge eating disorder, bulimia nervosa and anorexia nervosa, and describe their typical age of onset.
  2. Describe aetiological factors, including genetic loading, perfectionism, dieting and the maintaining effects of starvation.
  3. Distinguish anorexia nervosa, bulimia nervosa, binge eating disorder and ARFID by what drives the eating behaviour.
  4. Assess medical risk using BMI, observations, blood tests and the sit-up-squat-stand test, following the MEED guidance.
  5. Outline NICE psychological therapies, such as CBT-ED, MANTRA and family therapy for young people, and safe refeeding to prevent refeeding syndrome.
  6. State that anorexia nervosa has the highest mortality in psychiatry, with about one in five deaths due to suicide.
  7. Answer the chapter's 56 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
