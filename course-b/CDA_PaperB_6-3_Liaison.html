Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare candidates to answer MRCPsych Paper B questions on assessing and managing mental disorder in people with physical illness.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Describe the raised rates of depression and anxiety in physical illness and list organic causes to exclude first.
  2. Choose safer psychotropics in hepatic, renal and cardiac disease, considering clearance, QTc prolongation and drug interactions.
  3. Outline how to answer a medical team's referral, including assessing capacity under the Mental Capacity Act 2005.
  4. Describe psychiatric effects of stroke, head injury and epilepsy in relation to lesion site, time since injury and seizures.
  5. Recognise red flags for Wilson's disease, Huntington's disease, multiple sclerosis and autoimmune encephalitis presenting with psychiatric symptoms.
  6. Explain positive-sign diagnosis and management of functional and medically unexplained symptoms, and distinguish normal grief from prolonged grief disorder.
  7. Answer the chapter's 64 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
