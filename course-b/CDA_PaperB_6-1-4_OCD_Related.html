Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare candidates to answer MRCPsych Paper B questions on obsessive-compulsive and related disorders.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. State the lifetime prevalence of OCD (about 2 to 3%) and its roughly equal sex ratio in adults.
  2. Explain genetic, cortico-striato-thalamo-cortical and cognitive models of OCD, including negative reinforcement by compulsions.
  3. Distinguish obsessions from compulsions and recognise BDD, hypochondriasis, olfactory reference disorder and hoarding disorder in ICD-11.
  4. Outline NICE stepped care: CBT with exposure and response prevention, then SSRIs or clomipramine at higher doses for at least 12 weeks.
  5. Describe the usual chronic, fluctuating course of OCD and the risk of relapse when medication is stopped early.
  6. Answer the chapter's 55 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
