Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to recognise, explain and manage tic disorders, including Tourette syndrome, for MRCPsych Paper B.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. State that Tourette syndrome affects about 1 in 100 children, with boys outnumbering girls about three or four to one.
  2. Explain the role of inherited differences in cortico-striato-thalamo-cortical circuits in tics, and of stress in worsening them.
  3. Distinguish provisional tic disorder, chronic motor or vocal tic disorder and Tourette syndrome by duration and tic type.
  4. Outline first-line behavioural therapy (CBIT, habit reversal) and medicines such as aripiprazole and clonidine.
  5. Describe the typical course (onset at five to seven, peak at ten to twelve) and the impact of OCD and ADHD.
  6. Answer the chapter's 64 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
