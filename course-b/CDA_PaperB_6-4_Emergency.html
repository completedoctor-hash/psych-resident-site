Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to assess and manage psychiatric emergencies safely, effectively and lawfully for MRCPsych Paper B.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Explain why NICE NG225 advises against risk scales to predict suicide, and construct a collaborative safety plan with rapid follow-up.
  2. Describe Caplan's crisis theory and the role of crisis resolution and home treatment teams in avoiding admission.
  3. Distinguish delirium, intoxication, neuroleptic malignant syndrome and serotonin syndrome, starting with glucose and vital signs.
  4. Outline NICE NG10 rapid tranquillisation, including oral medication first and intramuscular lorazepam or haloperidol with promethazine.
  5. Apply the Mental Capacity Act 2005, common law or Mental Health Act sections 135 and 136 to an emergency vignette.
  6. Answer the chapter's 81 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
