Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to explain how UK services for people with intellectual disability developed and how common intellectual disability and its comorbidities are for MRCPsych Paper B.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Explain Nirje's normalisation principle and Wolfensberger's social role valorisation, giving one service example of each.
  2. Outline the move from long-stay hospitals to community care, including the Winterbourne View scandal and the Transforming Care programme.
  3. State the prevalence of intellectual disability (about 1 to 2%) and the proportion of people with mild disability.
  4. List common added impairments in intellectual disability, including epilepsy, sensory loss and cerebral palsy, and describe how epilepsy rises with severity.
  5. Explain the biological, psychological and social reasons why mental illness is more common, and define diagnostic overshadowing.
  6. Answer the chapter's 41 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
