Aim and objectives

Aim. To explain the pharmacology, effects and epidemiology of alcohol, cannabis, opioids, sedatives and stimulants for MRCPsych Paper B.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Describe alcohol metabolism by alcohol and aldehyde dehydrogenase, its zero-order elimination, and its actions at GABA-A and NMDA receptors.
  2. Distinguish the effects of THC and CBD, and explain why synthetic cannabinoids acting as full CB1 agonists are more dangerous than cannabis.
  3. Explain mu-opioid receptor agonism and recognise the overdose triad of pinpoint pupils, reduced consciousness and respiratory depression.
  4. Compare benzodiazepines, Z-drugs, barbiturates and GHB by their action on GABA-A receptors and their risk of dangerous withdrawal.
  5. Describe how cocaine blocks and amphetamines reverse monoamine reuptake, and how caffeine antagonises adenosine receptors.
  6. Answer the chapter's 40 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
