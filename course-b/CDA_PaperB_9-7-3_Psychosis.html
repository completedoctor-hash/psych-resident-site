Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to recognise and manage psychotic disorders in children and adolescents for MRCPsych Paper B.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Describe the rarity of psychosis before 13, its steep rise through adolescence and earlier onset in boys.
  2. Explain aetiology, including genetic and neurodevelopmental vulnerability, cannabis use and adversity.
  3. Recognise the insidious presentation of early-onset psychosis and list differentials such as organic causes, autism and trauma.
  4. Outline NICE treatment: an antipsychotic chosen with the young person by side-effect profile, plus family intervention and CBT for psychosis.
  5. Describe physical health monitoring, including weight, glucose, lipids and prolactin.
  6. List predictors of poor outcome, such as early and insidious onset, and describe good transition care.
  7. Answer the chapter's 44 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
