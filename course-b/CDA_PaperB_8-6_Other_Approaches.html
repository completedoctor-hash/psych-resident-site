Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to describe interpersonal, narrative, structured personality disorder, humanistic, psychedelic and digital therapies for MRCPsych Paper B.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Describe IPT's four problem areas (grief, role disputes, role transitions, interpersonal deficits) and its adaptations IPT-A and IPSRT.
  2. Compare narrative therapy (White and Epston), open dialogue and solution-focused therapy, including the miracle question.
  3. Distinguish transference-focused psychotherapy (Kernberg), structured clinical management and good psychiatric management for borderline personality disorder.
  4. Name Rogers' three core conditions of person-centred therapy and outline arts, body and nature based approaches.
  5. Outline evidence for and risks of psilocybin- and MDMA-assisted psychotherapy, including set and setting.
  6. Compare guided and unguided digital CBT and explain how the therapeutic alliance works in remote therapy.
  7. Answer the chapter's 60 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
