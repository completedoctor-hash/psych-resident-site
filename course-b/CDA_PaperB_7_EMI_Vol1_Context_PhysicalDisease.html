Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you for MRCPsych Paper B questions on ageing, old age services, assessment and the psychiatric aspects of physical disease.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Describe population ageing and explain why dementia numbers rise even as age-specific risk falls.
  2. Outline the Care Act 2014, means-tested social care, and the roles of carers and the voluntary sector.
  3. Describe old age liaison work in the general hospital and the effects of frailty and polypharmacy.
  4. Outline a comprehensive old age assessment, including collateral history, cognitive testing and capacity.
  5. Describe psychiatric features of Parkinson's disease, Huntington's disease and Parkinson-plus disorders, including impulse control disorders on dopamine agonists.
  6. Recognise post-stroke depression, Charles Bonnet syndrome and treatable causes of cognitive decline, such as B12 deficiency and hypothyroidism.
  7. Answer the chapter's 114 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
