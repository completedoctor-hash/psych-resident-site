Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to recognise, treat and predict the course of anxiety and fear-related disorders for MRCPsych Paper B.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Compare the prevalence of generalised anxiety disorder, specific phobia, social anxiety and panic disorder in England.
  2. Explain how avoidance maintains fear, and outline Clark's cognitive model of panic as catastrophic misinterpretation of bodily sensations.
  3. Distinguish GAD, panic disorder, agoraphobia, social anxiety disorder and specific phobia by the focus of fear, its timing and avoidance.
  4. Apply NICE stepped care for GAD and panic disorder, naming sertraline as the first-line drug for GAD and avoiding long-term benzodiazepines.
  5. Describe the usual chronic, fluctuating course of anxiety disorders and list factors such as comorbid depression that predict a poorer outcome.
  6. Answer the chapter's 55 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
