Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare candidates for MRCPsych Paper B EMI questions on psychosis, mood, anxiety, OCD, attachment and eating disorders in young people.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Outline treatment of early onset psychosis with an antipsychotic plus family intervention and CBT, and list poor prognostic factors.
  2. Recognise depression in young people presenting with irritability, and state that fluoxetine is only added to psychological therapy.
  3. Outline treatment of anxiety and OCD in young people, including family-involved exposure and response prevention and when to add an SSRI.
  4. Distinguish reactive attachment disorder from disinhibited social engagement disorder and explain why coercive therapies are not used.
  5. Recognise anorexia, bulimia and ARFID in young people, and name family therapy as first-line treatment for anorexia under 18.
  6. Interpret medical risk in a young person with an eating disorder using the MEED guidance.
  7. Answer the chapter's 228 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
