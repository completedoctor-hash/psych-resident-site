Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to recognise, distinguish and manage delirium, mild neurocognitive disorder and amnestic disorder for MRCPsych Paper B.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Compare how common delirium, mild cognitive impairment (about one in six over 65) and pure amnestic syndromes are.
  2. Explain delirium as a trigger acting on a vulnerable brain, and link amnestic disorder to damage in the Papez circuit.
  3. Distinguish delirium, mild cognitive impairment and amnestic disorder by onset, attention and daily function, using the 4AT or CAM.
  4. Outline NICE delirium prevention and management, using short-term haloperidol only as a last resort.
  5. Describe outcomes, including higher mortality and dementia risk after delirium and why many people with MCI stay stable or improve.
  6. Answer the chapter's 70 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
