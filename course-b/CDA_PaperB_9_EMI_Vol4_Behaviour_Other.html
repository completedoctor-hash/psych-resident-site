Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to answer extended matching questions on youth substance use, conduct problems, other childhood disorders, transitions and risk for MRCPsych Paper B.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Describe trends in youth substance use: falling drinking and smoking, rising vaping, and cannabis as the commonest illicit drug.
  2. Outline layered risk factors for adolescent substance use and why treatment combines talking, family and school work, harm reduction and safeguarding.
  3. Distinguish oppositional defiant disorder from conduct disorder and choose parent training as first-line treatment.
  4. Recognise wetting, soiling and childhood sleep problems, and outline first-line management with explanation, enuresis alarms, laxatives and sleep plans.
  5. Apply principles of transition planning, inpatient admission and self-harm assessment to a young person in a vignette.
  6. Answer the chapter's 140 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
