Aim and objectives

Aim. To explain why people start, continue and recover from substance use, using biopsychosocial and trauma-informed models, for MRCPsych Paper B.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Describe how genetic risk, early adversity, peers, price and availability contribute to starting substance use, giving one example of each.
  2. Explain the shift from positive to negative reinforcement as substance use moves from pleasure-seeking to dependence.
  3. Describe how adverse childhood experiences and trauma raise the risk of substance use, and outline the principles of trauma-informed care.
  4. Define recovery capital and list its personal, social and community components.
  5. Outline Marlatt and Gordon's relapse prevention model, including high-risk situations and the abstinence violation effect.
  6. Answer the chapter's 24 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
