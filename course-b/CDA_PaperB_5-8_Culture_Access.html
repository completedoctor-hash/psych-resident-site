Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to deliver culturally competent, accessible psychiatric care that accounts for culture, spirituality, migration and inequality, for MRCPsych Paper B.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Explain how culture shapes presentation and help seeking, using Kleinman's explanatory models and the DSM-5 Cultural Formulation Interview.
  2. Describe how to take a spiritual history and distinguish culturally accepted religious experience from psychopathology.
  3. Outline mental health risks for refugees and asylum seekers, including pre-migration trauma, post-migration stress and barriers to care.
  4. Describe UK ethnic inequalities, including higher rates of Mental Health Act detention among Black people and AESOP findings on psychosis.
  5. Explain Link and Phelan's model of stigma, which depends on power, and name one UK anti-stigma programme such as Time to Change.
  6. Answer the chapter's 52 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
