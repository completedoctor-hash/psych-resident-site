Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to recognise, assess and manage drug use disorders for MRCPsych Paper B.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Outline a structured drug history covering substance, amount, route, frequency and reasons, and choose a suitable confirmatory urine or oral fluid test.
  2. Identify the likely drug in an intoxication vignette using pupils, pulse, blood pressure, temperature and one distinguishing sign.
  3. Compare withdrawal from opioids, stimulants, benzodiazepines and GHB, and explain why benzodiazepine and GHB withdrawal can be life-threatening.
  4. Describe opioid substitution with methadone and buprenorphine, and the role of take-home naloxone in preventing overdose deaths.
  5. Apply harm reduction and psychosocial interventions, such as contingency management, to a drug use case.
  6. Answer the chapter's 32 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
