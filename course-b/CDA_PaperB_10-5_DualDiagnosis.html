Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to assess and manage people with co-occurring substance use, mental disorder and physical multimorbidity for MRCPsych Paper B.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Distinguish substance-induced psychosis or depression from a primary disorder using the timing of onset and the course during abstinence.
  2. Explain the self-medication, shared vulnerability and causal models linking cannabis with psychosis and alcohol with depression.
  3. Describe how substance use interacts with anxiety, PTSD, personality disorder and ADHD, and outline integrated treatment for each.
  4. Apply the 'no wrong door' principle and NICE NG58 guidance to decide which service leads care in a clinical vignette.
  5. Describe the physical multimorbidity common in dual diagnosis, including blood-borne viruses, liver disease and smoking-related illness.
  6. Answer the chapter's 25 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
