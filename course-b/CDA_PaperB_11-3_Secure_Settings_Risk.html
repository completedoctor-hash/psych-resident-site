Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to explain how secure services, prisons and risk assessment tools work for mentally disordered offenders, for MRCPsych Paper B.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Distinguish physical, procedural and relational security, giving one ward example of each.
  2. Outline the pathway through high, medium and low secure care, naming England's three high secure hospitals: Broadmoor, Rampton and Ashworth.
  3. Describe the prevalence of mental disorder in prisons, why suicide risk peaks early in custody, and transfer under sections 47 and 48.
  4. Describe the HCR-20 and the 20-item PCL-R, and explain why risk tools must not give an individual a percentage risk.
  5. Explain conditional discharge of restricted patients under section 37/41 and the role of MAPPA in community supervision.
  6. Answer the chapter's 72 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
