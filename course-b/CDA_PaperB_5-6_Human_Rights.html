Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to apply capacity, consent, confidentiality and human rights law to psychiatric practice in the UK for MRCPsych Paper B.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. List the five principles of the Mental Capacity Act 2005 and apply the four-part functional test of capacity in a vignette.
  2. Define the three requirements of valid consent: adequate information, voluntariness and capacity.
  3. Outline the safeguards for seclusion and restraint in the Mental Health Act Code of Practice and the Mental Health Units (Use of Force) Act 2018.
  4. Explain when confidentiality may be breached in the public interest, citing Tarasoff and W v Egdell.
  5. Describe Articles 3, 5 and 8 of the European Convention on Human Rights, given effect in UK law by the Human Rights Act 1998.
  6. Explain the Bournewood case (HL v UK) and the Cheshire West 'acid test' for deprivation of liberty.
  7. Answer the chapter's 49 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
