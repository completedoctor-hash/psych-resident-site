Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you for MRCPsych Paper B questions on family adversity, child services, child protection and physical health in child psychiatry.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Explain the effects of parental mental illness, household dysfunction and adverse childhood experiences on child development.
  2. Describe how CAMHS is organised, from school-based support to inpatient care, and how outcomes are measured.
  3. Outline the legal routes of children's social care, including section 17 and section 47 of the Children Act 1989.
  4. Recognise the four categories of child abuse and newer forms, such as child sexual and criminal exploitation.
  5. Describe the psychiatrist's safeguarding duty to notice, record, refer and share information.
  6. Identify physical causes of psychiatric presentations in children, such as thyroid disease, encephalitis and medication effects.
  7. Answer the chapter's 78 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
