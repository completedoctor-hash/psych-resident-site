Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to answer extended matching questions on psychotherapy service delivery, group therapies, other approaches and psychotherapeutic psychiatry for MRCPsych Paper B.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Distinguish specific from common factors in therapy, and efficacy from effectiveness in outcome research.
  2. Describe the dose-response curve and stepped care, and match therapies to disorders using NICE guidance.
  3. Name Yalom's eleven therapeutic factors and Bion's three basic assumptions: dependency, fight-flight and pairing.
  4. Describe Foulkes' group analysis, therapeutic communities and Menzies Lyth's social defences against anxiety in organisations.
  5. Outline IPT, open dialogue and guided digital therapy as approaches outside psychodynamic, CBT and systemic models.
  6. Construct a 5 Ps formulation and describe the roles of supervision and Balint groups in clinician wellbeing.
  7. Answer the chapter's 104 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
