Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to evaluate how psychological therapy services are delivered, measured and made accessible in the NHS for MRCPsych Paper B.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Distinguish specific from common factors, and explain the therapeutic alliance and the Dodo bird verdict.
  2. Describe the dose-effect curve of psychotherapy and how stepped care offers the lightest effective intervention first.
  3. Distinguish efficacy from effectiveness studies and compare control conditions such as waiting list and treatment as usual.
  4. Apply NICE guidelines to choose a psychological therapy by disorder, severity, age and previous treatment.
  5. Outline patient involvement through shared decision making, patient-reported outcome measures such as CORE-OM, and co-production of services.
  6. Describe NHS Talking Therapies (formerly IAPT), including self-referral, step 2 and step 3 care and session-by-session PHQ-9 and GAD-7 monitoring.
  7. Answer the chapter's 37 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
