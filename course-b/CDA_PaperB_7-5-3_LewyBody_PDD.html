Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to diagnose, treat and predict the course of dementia with Lewy bodies and Parkinson's disease dementia for MRCPsych Paper B.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. State that Lewy body disease is the second commonest degenerative dementia after Alzheimer's disease, and explain why it is underdiagnosed.
  2. Explain the role of misfolded alpha-synuclein and apply the one-year rule to distinguish DLB from PDD.
  3. List the four core features of DLB (fluctuation, visual hallucinations, REM sleep behaviour disorder, parkinsonism) and name DaTscan as an indicative biomarker.
  4. Apply treatment principles: cholinesterase inhibitors first, reducing drugs that feed psychosis, and avoiding antipsychotics because of severe sensitivity reactions.
  5. Compare the course of DLB and PDD with Alzheimer's disease, including faster decline and risks from falls, dysphagia and delirium.
  6. Answer the chapter's 74 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
