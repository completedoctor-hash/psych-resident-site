Aim and objectives

Aim. To explain the psychiatrist's role at each stage of the criminal justice system in England and Wales for MRCPsych Paper B.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Outline the stages from arrest to sentence and describe the psychiatrist's role at each, from police station assessment to sentencing reports.
  2. Distinguish criminal responsibility (mental state at the offence) from fitness to plead (mental state at trial), and list the Pritchard criteria.
  3. Apply the M'Naghten rules to a vignette and distinguish insane from sane automatism.
  4. Describe the partial defences to murder: diminished responsibility, loss of control, infanticide and suicide pact.
  5. Outline diversion routes, including liaison and diversion services and Mental Health Act sections 35, 36, 37, 41, 47 and 48.
  6. Describe an expert witness's duties, including the overriding duty to the court and keeping fact separate from opinion.
  7. Answer the chapter's 83 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
