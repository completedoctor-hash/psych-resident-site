Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to work with the multidisciplinary team, behavioural phenotypes and families in intellectual disability for MRCPsych Paper B.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Outline the roles of each profession in a community learning disability team, including speech and language therapy, psychology and nursing.
  2. Match common syndromes to their behavioural phenotype, such as Prader-Willi with hyperphagia and fragile X with social anxiety.
  3. Recognise rarer syndromes from one clue: Smith-Magenis self-hugging, Lesch-Nyhan self-biting, Rett hand wringing, cri du chat cry, 22q11.2 deletion psychosis.
  4. Describe the family's response to a child's intellectual disability, including grief that returns at developmental milestones.
  5. Explain why challenging behaviour predicts carer stress more strongly than the severity of disability.
  6. Answer the chapter's 32 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
