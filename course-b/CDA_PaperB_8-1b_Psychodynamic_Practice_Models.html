Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to place psychodynamic therapies in stepped care, select suitable patients, and compare adapted models and their neuroscience for MRCPsych Paper B.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Explain where exploratory psychodynamic therapy sits in stepped care and in phased treatment of complex trauma.
  2. List selection criteria for brief psychodynamic therapy, including psychological mindedness, motivation and a focal problem, and describe Malan's two triangles.
  3. Match DIT, PIT, MBT, TFP and CAT to their target problems, length and signature technique, such as DIT's 16 sessions for depression.
  4. Describe the therapeutic community model and outline neuropsychoanalysis, including the work of Mark Solms.
  5. Explain Panksepp's primary emotional systems and how predictive processing and active inference describe the brain as a prediction machine.
  6. Answer the chapter's 49 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
