Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to answer extended matching questions on perinatal and reproductive psychiatry for MRCPsych Paper B.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Identify women at highest risk of perinatal illness, such as those with bipolar disorder or previous postpartum psychosis.
  2. Describe a perinatal risk assessment covering mother and baby, including suicide risk in the year after birth.
  3. Recognise postpartum psychosis as an emergency and outline same-day management, including mother and baby unit admission.
  4. Apply principles of prescribing in pregnancy and breastfeeding, including planning before conception and avoiding valproate.
  5. Distinguish bonding from attachment and describe how parental mental illness can affect the parent-infant relationship.
  6. Distinguish premenstrual dysphoric disorder from perimenopausal mood disorder, using a prospective symptom diary.
  7. Answer the chapter's 71 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
