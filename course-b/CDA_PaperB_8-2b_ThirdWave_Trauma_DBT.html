Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to explain third-wave CBT, trauma-focused therapies and dialectical behaviour therapy for MRCPsych Paper B.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Compare mindfulness-based cognitive therapy, compassion-focused therapy (Gilbert) and acceptance and commitment therapy (Hayes) by target and technique.
  2. Name the six core processes of the ACT hexaflex.
  3. Explain Ehlers and Clark's cognitive model of PTSD and compare trauma-focused CBT with EMDR.
  4. Outline phase-based treatment of complex PTSD: stabilisation, trauma processing and reintegration.
  5. Describe Linehan's DBT: the dialectic of acceptance and change, its four treatment modes and four skills modules.
  6. Answer the chapter's 46 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
