Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare candidates for MRCPsych Paper B EMI questions on psychosis, mood, anxiety, substance use and other disorders in later life.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Describe risk factors for very late-onset schizophrenia-like psychosis, including female sex, sensory impairment and social isolation.
  2. Outline treatment of late-life psychosis: build trust, correct hearing and sight, and start a low-dose antipsychotic.
  3. Recognise somatic, anxious and cognitive presentations of late-life depression, and distinguish depressive pseudodementia from dementia.
  4. Outline treatment of depression and bipolar disorder in later life, including lithium monitoring and the role of ECT.
  5. Apply safe principles to late-life anxiety and substance misuse, including avoiding benzodiazepines and giving thiamine.
  6. Recognise prion disease, PML, HIV-associated neurocognitive disorder and hoarding disorder from course, signs and setting.
  7. Answer the chapter's 186 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
