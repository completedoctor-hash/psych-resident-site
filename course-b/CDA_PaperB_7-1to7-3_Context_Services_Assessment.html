Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to explain the demographic, service and assessment context of old age psychiatry in the UK for MRCPsych Paper B.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Describe UK and global population ageing, naming the oldest old (85 and over) as the fastest growing group and its effect on dementia numbers.
  2. Describe the roles of the multidisciplinary team and of liaison with physicians in detecting delirium, depression and dementia in older inpatients.
  3. Explain Care Act 2014 duties, including means-tested social care, carers' assessments and the role of the voluntary sector.
  4. Define frailty using Fried's five-criteria phenotype or the Clinical Frailty Scale, and explain polypharmacy and anticholinergic burden.
  5. Apply an informant history of change in daily function and cognitive tests such as ACE-III and MoCA in assessing an older person.
  6. Answer the chapter's 92 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
