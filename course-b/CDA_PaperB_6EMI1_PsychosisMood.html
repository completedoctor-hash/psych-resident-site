Aim and objectives

Aim. To build rapid recognition of schizophrenia, mood and personality disorders and their drug treatments so you can answer MRCPsych Paper B extended matching items accurately.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Apply ICD-11 criteria to diagnose schizophrenia, including the requirement for symptoms lasting at least one month.
  2. Explain the gene-environment causes of schizophrenia, depression and personality disorder, including the 40 to 50% heritability of personality disorder.
  3. Compare first- and second-generation antipsychotics by dose and side effects, and state that clozapine follows two adequate antipsychotic trials.
  4. List clozapine monitoring, including weekly full blood counts for the first 18 weeks, and its risks of myocarditis and constipation.
  5. Apply ICD-11 depression criteria (at least 5 of 10 symptoms for two weeks) and measure severity with scales such as PHQ-9 and HAM-D.
  6. Select antidepressants safely, recognising hyponatraemia and serotonin syndrome, and state lithium's usual maintenance range of 0.6 to 0.8 mmol/L.
  7. Answer the chapter's 124 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
