Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to assess and manage psychiatric emergencies for MRCPsych Paper B.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Explain why NICE (NG225) advises against using risk scales to predict suicide, and describe needs-based assessment with safety planning.
  2. Identify physical causes of acute agitation by checking glucose and vital signs first.
  3. Distinguish neuroleptic malignant syndrome, serotonin syndrome, delirium and catatonia in an emergency vignette.
  4. Describe the assessment of stupor and catatonia, including the lorazepam challenge.
  5. Outline rapid tranquillisation following NICE NG10, using the least restrictive option first.
  6. Choose between the Mental Capacity Act, the Mental Health Act and common law in an emergency scenario.
  7. Answer the chapter's 83 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
