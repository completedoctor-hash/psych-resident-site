Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to recognise, explain and treat obsessive-compulsive and related disorders in young people for MRCPsych Paper B.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. State that 1 to 2 in 100 young people have had OCD by the end of their teens, and explain why it often stays hidden.
  2. Explain OCD through inherited risk, cortico-striatal circuits, negative reinforcement by rituals and family accommodation.
  3. Distinguish OCD from normal childhood rituals, autistic repetitive behaviour and tics, and name the CY-BOCS.
  4. Outline NICE treatment: CBT with exposure and response prevention involving the family, adding an SSRI such as sertraline.
  5. Describe the outcome of childhood OCD, including about four in ten still having full OCD years later.
  6. Answer the chapter's 48 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
