Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare candidates to answer MRCPsych Paper B questions on substance use in young people.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Describe recent trends in drinking, smoking, vaping and cannabis use among young people in England.
  2. Explain individual, peer, family and adversity risk factors for starting and continuing substance use.
  3. Recognise indirect presentations of substance problems and screen using a tool such as the CRAFFT.
  4. Outline treatment: psychosocial and family interventions, harm reduction and safeguarding, with assessment of Gillick competence.
  5. Describe outcomes of early, heavy and daily use, including adult dependence and psychosis with cannabis.
  6. Answer the chapter's 42 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
