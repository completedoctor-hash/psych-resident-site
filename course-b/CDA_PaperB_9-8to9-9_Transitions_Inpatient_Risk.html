Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to manage transitions to adult care, inpatient admission and acute and chronic risk in young people for MRCPsych Paper B.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Explain why transition from CAMHS to adult services must be planned, given that most adult mental disorders start before 18.
  2. List indications and contraindications for adolescent inpatient admission, and describe its potential harms.
  3. Describe a psychosocial and safeguarding assessment after self-harm in a young person, in line with NICE NG225.
  4. Construct a safety plan for a young person and distinguish acute from chronic risk in a clinical vignette.
  5. Answer the chapter's 36 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
