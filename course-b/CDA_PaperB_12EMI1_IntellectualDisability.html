Aim and objectives

Aim. To build rapid recognition of services, genetic syndromes, behavioural phenotypes, assessment and treatment in intellectual disability so you can answer MRCPsych Paper B extended matching items accurately.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Outline the shift from institutions to needs-led community care and state the prevalence of intellectual disability (about 1 to 2%).
  2. Match Down syndrome (trisomy 21), fragile X syndrome (FMR1 CGG repeat expansion) and Prader-Willi syndrome (15q11-13) to their genetic cause.
  3. Describe functional analysis of behaviour that challenges and outline positive behaviour support.
  4. Apply STOMP principles to review antipsychotic prescribing for behaviour that challenges in the absence of mental illness.
  5. Recognise behavioural phenotypes, such as hyperphagia in Prader-Willi syndrome and overfriendliness with hyperacusis in Williams syndrome.
  6. Recognise rarer phenotypes: self-biting with high urate in Lesch-Nyhan syndrome, hand wringing in Rett syndrome and self-hugging in Smith-Magenis syndrome.
  7. Answer the chapter's 32 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
