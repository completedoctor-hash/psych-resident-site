Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare candidates to answer MRCPsych Paper B questions on core CBT techniques and CBT models for depression and anxiety.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Describe collaborative empiricism and Socratic dialogue, and give an example Socratic question.
  2. Outline the structure of a CBT session: agenda, homework review, psychoeducation and agreed goals.
  3. Name session-by-session outcome measures, such as the PHQ-9 and GAD-7, and interpret a change in score.
  4. Distinguish core beliefs (schemas), rules for living and negative automatic thoughts in Beck's cognitive model.
  5. Draw a five areas formulation linking situation, thoughts, emotions, physical sensations and behaviour.
  6. Describe CBT for depression (behavioural activation, cognitive triad) and for anxiety (exposure, safety behaviours, Clark's panic model).
  7. Answer the chapter's 62 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
