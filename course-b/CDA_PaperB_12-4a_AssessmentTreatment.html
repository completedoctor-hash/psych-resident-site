Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to assess, diagnose and treat mental disorder and behaviour that challenges in people with intellectual disability, for MRCPsych Paper B.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Describe adaptations to assessment in intellectual disability, including setting, communication aids, extra time and collateral history.
  2. Explain diagnostic overshadowing and recognise change from a person's baseline behaviour as a sign of mental illness.
  3. Name tools for measuring intellectual and adaptive functioning, such as the WAIS and Vineland scales.
  4. Apply functional analysis (antecedent, behaviour, consequence) and outline positive behaviour support for behaviour that challenges.
  5. Explain the aims of STOMP and when NICE supports antipsychotics for behaviour that challenges, alongside a behaviour plan.
  6. Describe how CBT and other therapies are adapted, using simpler language, pictures, shorter sessions and a supporter.
  7. Answer the chapter's 41 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
