Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to apply psychotherapeutic thinking to formulation, prescribing, clinician wellbeing, public health and ethics in psychiatric practice for MRCPsych Paper B.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Construct a collaborative formulation explaining why this person, why now and what maintains the problem, while tolerating uncertainty.
  2. Explain how meaning, trust and placebo and nocebo effects shape prescribing and deprescribing decisions.
  3. Describe how supervision, reflective practice and Balint groups protect clinicians and teams from burnout.
  4. Outline psychotherapy as public health, including prevention of childhood adversity and trauma-informed services.
  5. Explain power imbalance, boundary violations and diversity in therapy, and apply professional ethics and law to a clinical scenario.
  6. Answer the chapter's 43 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
