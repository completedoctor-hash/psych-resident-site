Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to recognise, treat and predict the outcome of depression and bipolar disorder in older people for MRCPsych Paper B.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Compare the prevalence of major and milder depression in later life with younger adults, and identify high-risk groups such as care home residents.
  2. Explain the vascular depression hypothesis and other causes, including physical illness, loss and isolation.
  3. Recognise atypical presentations of late-life depression, such as somatic complaints, anxiety and cognitive impairment, and screen with the Geriatric Depression Scale.
  4. Apply treatment principles in older adults: lower starting doses, sodium monitoring with SSRIs, ECT for psychotic depression and longer continuation after remission.
  5. Describe outcomes of late-life depression, including relapse, chronicity, physical decline and increased risk of dementia.
  6. Answer the chapter's 66 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
