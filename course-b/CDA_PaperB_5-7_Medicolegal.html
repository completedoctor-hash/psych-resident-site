Aim and objectives

Aim. To explain safeguarding, financial management, driving and civil capacity for MRCPsych Paper B.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. List the ten types of abuse and neglect in the Care Act 2014 guidance and describe the section 42 safeguarding enquiry duty.
  2. Compare lasting power of attorney, Court of Protection deputyship and DWP appointeeship for managing a person's finances.
  3. Apply DVLA guidance to advise a patient with psychosis, bipolar disorder or dementia about driving and notification.
  4. Apply the Mental Capacity Act 2005 functional test (understand, retain, use or weigh, communicate) to a decision in a vignette.
  5. Describe capacity for specific civil decisions, including testamentary capacity under Banks v Goodfellow.
  6. Answer the chapter's 48 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
