Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to apply psychotherapeutic thinking to everyday psychiatric practice for MRCPsych Paper B.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Describe how presentations differ across inpatient, community, age and cultural settings, and how to adapt therapy while keeping its active ingredient.
  2. Explain how to build and repair a therapeutic alliance and manage transitions and endings of care.
  3. Distinguish Kernberg's neurotic, borderline and psychotic levels of personality organisation, and ICD-11 mild, moderate and severe personality disorder.
  4. Compare models of mourning, such as Bowlby's phases and the dual process model, and describe ICD-11 prolonged grief disorder.
  5. Outline management of bodily distress that limits investigations and builds one relationship and one coordinated plan.
  6. Describe psychological approaches to self-harm, suicide and violence, and their impact on the family and team.
  7. Answer the chapter's 49 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
