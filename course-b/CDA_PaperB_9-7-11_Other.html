Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to assess and manage wetting, soiling, sleep and other childhood disorders for MRCPsych Paper B.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Describe how common enuresis, encopresis and childhood sleep problems are, and how they decline with age.
  2. Explain maturational, stress and family factors in wetting, soiling and sleep problems.
  3. Distinguish primary from secondary enuresis, and retentive from non-retentive soiling.
  4. Distinguish night terrors from nightmares by timing in the night and recall the next morning.
  5. Outline treatment with enuresis alarms, desmopressin, laxatives and behavioural sleep plans.
  6. Describe outcomes, including effects on confidence, schooling and family life.
  7. Answer the chapter's 52 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
