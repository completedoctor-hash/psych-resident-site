Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you for MRCPsych Paper B questions on crime and mental disorder, the criminal justice system and secure care.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Match common offence types to their usual associated conditions, such as alcohol with arson and drug dependence with acquisitive crime.
  2. Describe how age, sex, victimisation and suggestibility shape offending, and the reasonable adjustments required by the Equality Act 2010.
  3. Distinguish criminal responsibility from fitness to plead, and list the Pritchard criteria.
  4. Compare full defences (insanity, automatism) with partial defences to murder: diminished responsibility, loss of control, infanticide and suicide pact.
  5. Outline diversion from custody, and the principles of writing a court report and giving oral evidence.
  6. Describe physical, procedural and relational security, and compare actuarial tools with structured professional judgement such as the HCR-20.
  7. Answer the chapter's 148 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
