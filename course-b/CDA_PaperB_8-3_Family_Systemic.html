Aim and objectives

Aim. To explain the theory, schools and techniques of family and systemic therapy for MRCPsych Paper B.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Describe systems theory concepts, such as circular causality and homeostasis, that underpin family therapy.
  2. Compare the structural (Minuchin), strategic (Haley) and Milan schools of family therapy.
  3. Match techniques to schools: enactment and unbalancing (structural), task setting and paradox (strategic), circular questioning and neutrality (Milan).
  4. Outline attachment-informed family therapy and its aim of restoring safety within the family.
  5. Describe multisystemic therapy and functional family therapy for serious adolescent offending.
  6. Explain expressed emotion (criticism, hostility, emotional over-involvement) and describe family intervention for psychosis.
  7. Answer the chapter's 48 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
