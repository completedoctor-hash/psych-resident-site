Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare candidates to answer MRCPsych Paper B questions on ADHD and other neurodevelopmental disorders.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. State that ADHD affects about one child in twenty, with more boys than girls diagnosed.
  2. Describe the high heritability of ADHD and early-life risks such as prematurity and prenatal exposures.
  3. Apply ICD-11 features of ADHD, including onset before age 12 and symptoms in more than one setting.
  4. Recognise tic disorders, Tourette syndrome, developmental coordination disorder and specific learning disorders in a vignette.
  5. Outline NICE treatment order for children: methylphenidate, then lisdexamfetamine, then atomoxetine or guanfacine, with height, weight, pulse and blood pressure checks.
  6. Describe outcomes, including persistence into adulthood and risks of injury, offending and substance misuse.
  7. Answer the chapter's 92 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
