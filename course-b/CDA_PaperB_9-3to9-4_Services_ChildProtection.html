Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to work within CAMHS, partner agencies and safeguarding systems to protect children for MRCPsych Paper B.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Describe how CAMHS is organised, including the THRIVE framework, and the roles within its multidisciplinary team.
  2. Outline Children Act 1989 routes into social care, including Section 17 (child in need) and Section 47 (child protection enquiries).
  3. Describe joint working with paediatrics, with one clear lead clinician, for problems such as eating disorders and functional symptoms.
  4. Outline how children's needs for safety, care and a reliable adult change from infancy to late adolescence.
  5. List the four categories of child abuse and recognise newer forms, including child sexual and criminal exploitation and fabricated or induced illness.
  6. Apply the psychiatrist's safeguarding duties under Working Together to Safeguard Children: notice, record, share information and refer.
  7. Answer the chapter's 63 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
