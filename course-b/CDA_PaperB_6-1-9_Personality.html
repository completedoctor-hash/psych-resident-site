Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare candidates to answer MRCPsych Paper B questions on the epidemiology, causes, diagnosis, treatment and outcome of personality disorders.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. State the approximate prevalence of personality disorder (about one adult in eight) and the types typical of community, inpatient and prison settings.
  2. Describe temperamental, genetic (heritability about 40 to 50%) and childhood adversity factors in personality disorder.
  3. Apply the ICD-11 model: severity rating and the five trait domains, plus the borderline pattern specifier.
  4. Recognise the older named personality disorder types from a clinical vignette.
  5. Compare DBT and MBT for borderline personality disorder and explain why NICE does not recommend drugs for the disorder itself.
  6. Describe outcome, including remission of borderline personality disorder within ten years and persisting suicide risk.
  7. Answer the chapter's 52 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
