Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to answer extended matching questions on suicide, personality, therapy, bereavement, sleep and sexual health in older people for MRCPsych Paper B.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. List risk factors that raise suicide risk after self-harm in older people, such as depression, chronic pain and loneliness.
  2. Recognise late-onset personality change in an older person and explain why an organic cause must be excluded.
  3. Describe adaptations of pace, format and therapist stance in psychotherapy with older people.
  4. Distinguish normal grief, prolonged grief disorder, depression and adjustment disorder in a clinical vignette.
  5. Recognise the causes of insomnia in older people and choose CBT for insomnia before a hypnotic.
  6. Answer the chapter's 35 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
