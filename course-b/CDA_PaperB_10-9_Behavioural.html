Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to recognise and manage gambling, gaming and other non-substance addictive behaviours for MRCPsych Paper B.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Describe the ICD-11 features of gambling disorder: impaired control, increasing priority over other activities, and continuation despite harm.
  2. Explain how variable-ratio reinforcement and near-misses maintain gambling behaviour.
  3. Outline screening with the Problem Gambling Severity Index and treatment with cognitive behavioural therapy.
  4. Distinguish gaming disorder from heavy but healthy gaming using ICD-11 criteria, normally evident over at least 12 months.
  5. Recognise compulsive sexual behaviour disorder and state its ICD-11 classification as an impulse control disorder.
  6. Answer the chapter's 16 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
