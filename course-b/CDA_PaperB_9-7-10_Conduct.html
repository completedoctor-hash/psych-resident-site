Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to explain the epidemiology, causes, presentation, treatment and outcome of conduct disorders for MRCPsych Paper B.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Describe the prevalence of conduct problems, including higher rates in boys, deprived households and looked-after children.
  2. Explain child and family risk factors, including harsh, inconsistent parenting and Patterson's coercive cycle.
  3. Distinguish oppositional defiant disorder from conduct-dissocial disorder and recognise the limited prosocial emotions specifier.
  4. Outline NICE CG158 treatment, including group parent training and multisystemic therapy, and the limited role of medication.
  5. Compare Moffitt's life-course-persistent and adolescence-limited antisocial behaviour and their adult outcomes.
  6. Answer the chapter's 67 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
