Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to recognise, assess and manage harmful use of hallucinogens, dissociatives, nicotine, inhalants, MDMA, novel psychoactive substances, misused medicines and IPEDs for MRCPsych Paper B.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Explain how classic hallucinogens act at 5-HT2A receptors and ketamine blocks NMDA receptors, and describe ketamine-related bladder damage.
  2. Compare NICE-recommended smoking cessation treatments: nicotine replacement therapy, varenicline, bupropion and e-cigarettes.
  3. Recognise the acute dangers of volatile inhalant use, including sudden sniffing death from cardiac arrhythmia.
  4. Explain how MDMA causes hyperthermia and hyponatraemia, and why novel psychoactive substances are often missed by standard drug screens.
  5. Name commonly misused prescribed and over-the-counter medicines, including codeine, gabapentinoids and benzodiazepines, and describe the psychiatric effects of anabolic steroids.
  6. Answer the chapter's 32 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
