Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to recognise, explain and manage bipolar disorder for MRCPsych Paper B.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. State the lifetime prevalence of bipolar I disorder (about 1%), its young age of onset and its equal sex ratio.
  2. Describe the high heritability of bipolar disorder from twin studies, and triggers such as sleep loss, stress, some drugs and childbirth.
  3. Distinguish mania from hypomania by duration (seven versus four days in DSM-5), psychotic features and functional impairment.
  4. Outline NICE treatment: an antipsychotic for mania, quetiapine for bipolar depression, and lithium for long-term prevention.
  5. Explain why antidepressant monotherapy is avoided in bipolar disorder.
  6. Describe the course of bipolar disorder, including more time spent depressed than elevated and excess physical health mortality.
  7. Answer the chapter's 75 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
