Aim and objectives

Aim. To explain group therapies, therapeutic communities and unconscious processes in organisations for MRCPsych Paper B.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. List Yalom's therapeutic factors, such as universality, instillation of hope and cohesiveness, and identify them in a group vignette.
  2. Describe Bion's basic assumptions (dependency, fight-flight, pairing) and contrast them with the work group.
  3. Compare supportive, skills-based and group-analytic (Foulkes) groups by aim and leader role.
  4. Describe Rapoport's four principles of therapeutic communities: democratisation, permissiveness, communalism and reality confrontation.
  5. Explain Menzies Lyth's social defences against anxiety in nursing organisations.
  6. Apply therapeutic principles, such as enabling and psychologically informed environments, to the design of wards, hostels and prisons.
  7. Answer the chapter's 54 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
