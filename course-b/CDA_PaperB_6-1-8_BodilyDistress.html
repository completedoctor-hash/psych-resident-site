Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to recognise, explain and manage bodily distress disorder and disorders of bodily experience for MRCPsych Paper B.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. State how often unexplained bodily symptoms occur in new hospital clinic patients, and compare this with the prevalence of bodily distress disorder.
  2. Explain the predisposing, precipitating and perpetuating factors in bodily distress, such as infection, symptom-focused worry and avoidance.
  3. Distinguish bodily distress disorder, hypochondriasis, body integrity dysphoria, functional neurological disorder, factitious disorder and malingering in a vignette.
  4. Outline management with one named clinician, scheduled appointments, limited investigations and CBT.
  5. Name predictors of a poor outcome, including many symptoms at onset and untreated depression.
  6. Answer the chapter's 39 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
