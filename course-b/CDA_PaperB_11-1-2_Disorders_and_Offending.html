Aim and objectives

Aim. To explain how specific mental disorders relate to offending behaviour for MRCPsych Paper B.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Explain how ADHD, autism and intellectual disability each link to offending, through impulsivity, special interests, naivety or suggestibility.
  2. Describe the modest link between schizophrenia and violence, and the multiplying effect of comorbid substance misuse.
  3. Recognise offences linked to depression, such as homicide-suicide and altruistic filicide, and to mania, such as disinhibited offending.
  4. Explain why substance use is the strongest mental health link to offending, through intoxication, funding a habit and the drug market.
  5. Describe how antisocial personality disorder develops from childhood conduct disorder, and define psychopathy using the PCL-R.
  6. Distinguish ictal from post-ictal violence in epilepsy, and outline the ICD-11 definition and treatment ladder for paraphilic disorders.
  7. Answer the chapter's 77 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
