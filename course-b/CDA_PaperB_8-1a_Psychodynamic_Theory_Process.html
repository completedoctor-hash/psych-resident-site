Aim and objectives

Aim. To explain psychodynamic theory, development and therapeutic process for MRCPsych Paper B.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Describe key concepts of Freud, Klein, Winnicott, Bion and Jung, such as the paranoid-schizoid position, the good-enough mother and containment.
  2. Explain how early attachment patterns form, pass between generations, and are shaped by adversity, culture and resilience.
  3. Describe the therapeutic alliance and frame, and why each makes it safe to explore old relationships.
  4. Classify defence mechanisms using Vaillant's psychotic, immature, neurotic and mature levels.
  5. Distinguish transference from countertransference and give a clinical example of each.
  6. Explain mechanisms of change in psychodynamic therapy, such as insight and working through.
  7. Answer the chapter's 61 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
