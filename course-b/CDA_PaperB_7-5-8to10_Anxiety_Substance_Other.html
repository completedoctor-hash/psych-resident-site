Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare candidates to answer MRCPsych Paper B questions on anxiety, substance use and rarer mental disorders in later life.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. State that anxiety disorders affect about one older person in ten and describe the roles of physical illness, medicines and loss.
  2. Explain why new-onset panic or OCD in later life needs an organic work-up.
  3. Outline treatment of late-life anxiety: review physical causes and medicines, adapted CBT and a low-dose SSRI, avoiding benzodiazepines.
  4. Distinguish early-onset from late-onset drinking in older people and describe screening with the AUDIT.
  5. Outline safe alcohol withdrawal in older people, including thiamine for Wernicke's encephalopathy and shorter-acting benzodiazepines.
  6. Recognise prion disease, progressive multifocal leukoencephalopathy, HIV-associated neurocognitive disorder and hoarding disorder from speed of onset and signs.
  7. Answer the chapter's 130 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
