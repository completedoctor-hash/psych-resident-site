Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare candidates for MRCPsych Paper B EMI questions on comorbidity, prescribing, psychosocial treatment and gambling in addictions psychiatry.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Distinguish substance-induced psychosis or depression from a primary disorder using the timeline and the course after a period of abstinence.
  2. Describe the 'no wrong door' principle for people with coexisting severe mental illness and substance misuse.
  3. Compare acamprosate, naltrexone and disulfiram for alcohol dependence, and methadone, buprenorphine and naltrexone for opioid dependence.
  4. Name Prochaska and DiClemente's stages of change and match each stage to a suitable intervention.
  5. Outline motivational interviewing skills (open questions, affirmations, reflections, summaries) and the role of change talk.
  6. Describe relapse prevention strategies and the features of gambling disorder, including variable-ratio reinforcement.
  7. Answer the chapter's 26 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
