Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to recognise, assess and manage harmful and dependent alcohol use, from withdrawal and detoxification to relapse prevention and physical harm, for MRCPsych Paper B.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Distinguish ICD-11 harmful pattern of alcohol use from alcohol dependence, and calculate units from a drink's volume and ABV.
  2. Describe an alcohol assessment that measures consumption, dependence (e.g. AUDIT, SADQ) and how safe it is to stop.
  3. Outline the timeline of alcohol withdrawal: tremor within hours, seizures within 48 hours and delirium tremens around days two to four.
  4. Describe a reducing chlordiazepoxide regimen with thiamine, and list criteria for choosing inpatient rather than community detoxification.
  5. Compare acamprosate, naltrexone and disulfiram for relapse prevention by mechanism, key contraindications and place in treatment.
  6. Recognise Wernicke's encephalopathy and explain why it is treated with parenteral thiamine on suspicion, before the full triad appears.
  7. Answer the chapter's 91 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
