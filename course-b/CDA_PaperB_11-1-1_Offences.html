Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to explain the links between specific offences and mental disorder, and their psychiatric relevance, for MRCPsych Paper B.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Describe the contribution of mental illness to homicide and state the criteria of the Infanticide Act 1938.
  2. Outline the associations of violence and domestic abuse with substance misuse, personality disorder and psychosis.
  3. Describe the psychiatric associations of arson, including pyromania, intellectual disability and alcohol misuse.
  4. Distinguish contact and non-contact sexual offending and name a sexual offence risk tool such as Risk Matrix 2000.
  5. Name Mullen's five types of stalker and distinguish morbid jealousy from erotomania (de Clérambault's syndrome).
  6. Describe the psychiatric associations of acquisitive offending, such as shoplifting in depression, dementia and kleptomania.
  7. Answer the chapter's 66 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
