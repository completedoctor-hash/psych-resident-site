Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to recognise, diagnose and support autistic children and young people and predict their outcomes for MRCPsych Paper B.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. State the prevalence of autism (about 1 to 2%) and explain why girls are underdiagnosed.
  2. Explain autism's heritability (about 80%), common and rare de novo variants, and the evidence against an MMR vaccine link.
  3. List the two core diagnostic domains and name ADOS-2 and ADI-R as structured assessment tools.
  4. Distinguish autism from look-alikes such as ADHD, language disorder, social anxiety and attachment difficulties in a vignette.
  5. Apply NICE guidance: psychosocial support first, melatonin for sleep, and antipsychotics only for severe behaviour that challenges.
  6. Name IQ and useful speech by about age five as predictors of adult outcome, and list later risks including epilepsy and suicide.
  7. Answer the chapter's 73 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
