Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to recognise and manage reactive attachment disorder and disinhibited social engagement disorder for MRCPsych Paper B.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Describe how rare attachment disorders are in ordinary families and how common they are after institutional care or neglect.
  2. Explain that attachment disorders are caused by grossly inadequate early care.
  3. Distinguish RAD from DSED, and both from autism, noting that both begin before the age of 5.
  4. Outline treatment through a stable, sensitive placement, and explain why coercive holding therapies must not be used.
  5. Describe outcomes: RAD usually fades with good care, while DSED often persists into adulthood.
  6. Answer the chapter's 41 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
