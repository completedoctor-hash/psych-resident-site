Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to explain the epidemiology, causes, diagnosis, treatment and course of depression for MRCPsych Paper B.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. State the point and lifetime prevalence of depression (about 1 in 20 and 1 in 6) and its higher rate in women.
  2. Describe causes of depression, including twin heritability of about 37%, early adversity and Brown and Harris's vulnerability factors.
  3. Apply ICD-11 criteria for a depressive episode (at least five of ten symptoms for two weeks) to a clinical vignette.
  4. Outline NICE NG222 treatment for less severe and more severe depression, including combined CBT and antidepressant.
  5. Describe the course of depression, including recurrence, residual symptoms as a relapse predictor, and continuing antidepressants after remission.
  6. Answer the chapter's 100 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
