Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to recognise and manage sleep-wake, catatonic, factitious and impulse control disorders for MRCPsych Paper B.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Compare the prevalence of insomnia, narcolepsy, catatonia and factitious disorder in community and clinical populations.
  2. Explain the loss of orexin (hypocretin) neurones in narcolepsy type 1 and the loss of REM atonia in REM sleep behaviour disorder.
  3. Distinguish NREM parasomnias early in the night from REM parasomnias late in the night in a clinical vignette.
  4. Recognise catatonia using the Bush-Francis Catatonia Rating Scale, and distinguish factitious disorder from malingering by motive.
  5. Apply first-line treatments: CBT for insomnia before hypnotics, and lorazepam then ECT for catatonia.
  6. Describe outcomes, including REM sleep behaviour disorder as an early warning of Parkinson's disease and dementia with Lewy bodies.
  7. Answer the chapter's 59 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
