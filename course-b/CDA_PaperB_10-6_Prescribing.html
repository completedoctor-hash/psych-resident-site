Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare candidates to answer MRCPsych Paper B questions on the law, medicines and overdose prevention in addictions prescribing.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Distinguish Misuse of Drugs Act 1971 classes A to C from Misuse of Drugs Regulations 2001 schedules 1 to 5, naming one drug in each.
  2. Compare methadone and buprenorphine for opioid substitution treatment by receptor action, overdose risk and retention in treatment.
  3. Outline when oral naltrexone is appropriate for preventing relapse after opioid detoxification.
  4. List the main periods of high opioid overdose risk, including starting treatment, leaving prison and loss of tolerance after detoxification.
  5. Describe take-home naloxone and supervised consumption as measures that reduce opioid deaths.
  6. Answer the chapter's 24 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
