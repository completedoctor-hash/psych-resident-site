Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to answer extended matching questions on perinatal, liaison and psychosexual psychiatry for MRCPsych Paper B.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Describe the spectrum from baby blues to postnatal depression (about 1 in 8) and postpartum psychosis, and identify women at highest risk.
  2. Apply principles of prescribing in pregnancy and breastfeeding, including avoiding valproate and never stopping treatment abruptly.
  3. Adjust psychotropic choice for liver, kidney and heart disease, and recognise endocrine and neurological mimics of psychiatric illness.
  4. Outline aetiological models of schizophrenia (dopamine, polygenic risk) and personality disorder (heritability about 40-50%).
  5. Classify sexual dysfunction by phase of the sexual response cycle and list common drug causes, such as SSRIs and prolactin-raising antipsychotics.
  6. Describe ICD-11 gender incongruence and explain why it is classified as a condition related to sexual health.
  7. Answer the chapter's 131 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
