Aim and objectives

Aim. To explain how population need for psychiatric services is assessed and how rehabilitation services meet it for MRCPsych Paper B.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Define need as the capacity to benefit from care, and distinguish need from demand and supply.
  2. Compare the epidemiological, comparative and corporate approaches to assessing a community's need for services.
  3. Describe Bradshaw's four types of need: normative, felt, expressed and comparative.
  4. Identify patients who need rehabilitation, such as those with persistent positive and negative symptoms, poor cognition and lost living skills.
  5. Describe the components of a rehabilitation service, from inpatient units to supported accommodation, and the recovery approach.
  6. Compare place-then-train (Individual Placement and Support) with train-then-place models of vocational rehabilitation.
  7. Answer the chapter's 46 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
