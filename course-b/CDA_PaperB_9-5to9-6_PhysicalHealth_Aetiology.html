Aim and objectives

Aim. To explain how physical illness and individual, family and social factors cause or shape mental disorder in young people for MRCPsych Paper B.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. List physical causes of new psychiatric symptoms in children, such as epilepsy, encephalitis, thyroid disease and medication effects.
  2. Explain how chronic illness such as diabetes raises the risk of mental disorder, and how mental disorder worsens adherence and outcome.
  3. Describe individual, family and social risk factors and explain how risks accumulate over time.
  4. Describe protective factors and resilience in children exposed to adversity.
  5. Explain gene-environment interaction using an example, such as MAOA genotype and childhood maltreatment.
  6. Answer the chapter's 32 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
