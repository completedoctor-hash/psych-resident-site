Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to recognise, investigate and manage vascular dementia for MRCPsych Paper B.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. State that vascular dementia is the second commonest dementia, accounting for about 15 to 20% of cases.
  2. Describe large- and small-vessel causes, hypertension as the leading modifiable risk, and CADASIL as a NOTCH3 inherited form.
  3. Distinguish vascular dementia from Alzheimer's disease by early executive slowing, stepwise course, focal signs, gait and bladder change.
  4. Interpret MRI findings such as white matter hyperintensities and lacunar infarcts.
  5. Outline treatment focused on preventing further stroke, and explain why NICE limits cholinesterase inhibitors to mixed dementia.
  6. Describe the stepwise course and reduced life expectancy due to cardiovascular disease.
  7. Answer the chapter's 67 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
