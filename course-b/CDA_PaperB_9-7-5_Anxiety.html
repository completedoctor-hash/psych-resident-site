Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare candidates to answer MRCPsych Paper B questions on anxiety, fear and trauma-related disorders in young people.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Describe typical ages of onset for separation anxiety, specific phobia, social anxiety and panic disorder.
  2. Explain the roles of behavioural inhibition, parental modelling and family accommodation in childhood anxiety.
  3. Distinguish normal developmental fears from anxiety disorders, and recognise selective mutism and PTSD in children.
  4. Outline treatment: CBT with graded exposure involving parents and school, trauma-focused CBT for PTSD, and SSRIs as a second step.
  5. Describe outcomes, including persistence and progression to depression and substance misuse.
  6. Answer the chapter's 70 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
