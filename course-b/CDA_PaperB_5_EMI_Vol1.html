Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you for MRCPsych Paper B questions on delivering mental health services, specific treatments, need, rights, law and culture.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Choose the correct team, such as crisis resolution, early intervention or assertive outreach, using age, diagnosis, urgency and engagement.
  2. Distinguish primary, secondary and tertiary prevention from Gordon's universal, selective and indicated prevention.
  3. Describe the indications for ECT, its twice-weekly delivery, dosing against seizure threshold, and Mental Health Act consent safeguards.
  4. Compare rTMS, deep brain stimulation, vagus nerve stimulation and neurosurgery for mental disorder by mechanism and indication.
  5. Outline Goldberg and Huxley's filters on the pathway to care, and Individual Placement and Support.
  6. Apply the Mental Capacity Act, ECHR Articles 3, 5 and 8, confidentiality rules and safeguarding duties to clinical scenarios.
  7. Answer the chapter's 119 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
