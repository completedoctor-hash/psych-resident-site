Aim and objectives

Aim. To explain how parental mental disorder and adverse childhood experiences affect child development for MRCPsych Paper B.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Explain how parental mental illness affects children through genes, parenting and family stress at each developmental stage.
  2. Describe the effects of domestic abuse, parental conflict, poverty and homelessness on child development and functioning.
  3. Explain how culture shapes explanations of distress and help-seeking, without lowering safeguarding thresholds.
  4. Describe the ACE study of Felitti and colleagues and the dose-response link between ACE count and later health.
  5. Explain why ACE scores describe group risk rather than one child's future, and name protective factors such as a reliable adult.
  6. Answer the chapter's 37 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
