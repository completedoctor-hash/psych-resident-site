Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to recognise, treat and predict the course of depression and bipolar disorder in children and adolescents for MRCPsych Paper B.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Describe how depression prevalence rises after puberty, when girls overtake boys, and why true bipolar disorder in young people is rare.
  2. Explain how genetic vulnerability, HPA axis stress responses and negative thinking style interact in adolescent depression.
  3. Recognise presentations of depression in young people, such as irritability, physical complaints, school avoidance and self-harm.
  4. Apply NICE NG134: psychological therapy first, with fluoxetine added only alongside therapy for moderate to severe depression.
  5. Name a second-generation antipsychotic, such as aripiprazole, as first-line treatment for adolescent mania.
  6. Describe the course: most recover from a first episode, but about half have another within a few years.
  7. Answer the chapter's 68 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
