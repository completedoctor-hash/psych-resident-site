Aim and objectives

Aim. To build rapid recognition of alcohol and drug use, intoxication, dependence and withdrawal so you can answer MRCPsych Paper B extended matching items accurately.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Match cannabis (CB1), opioids (mu), sedatives (GABA-A), stimulants (dopamine and noradrenaline) and hallucinogens (5-HT2A) to their main receptor actions.
  2. Calculate alcohol units (ml x ABV% / 1000) and distinguish hazardous, harmful and dependent drinking using AUDIT and the 14-unit weekly guideline.
  3. Assess a person who drinks for quantity, dependence severity (SADQ) and safety of withdrawal, including thiamine to prevent Wernicke's encephalopathy.
  4. Outline the alcohol withdrawal timeline: tremor within hours, seizures within 48 hours and delirium tremens around days two to three.
  5. Identify the drug in an intoxication vignette from pupils, pulse, blood pressure and temperature, such as pinpoint pupils with opioids.
  6. Compare drug withdrawal syndromes as mirror images of intoxication, such as dilated pupils, yawning and piloerection in opioid withdrawal.
  7. Answer the chapter's 32 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
