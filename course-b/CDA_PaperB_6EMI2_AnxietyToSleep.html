Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare candidates for MRCPsych Paper B EMI questions on anxiety, OCD, stress, bodily distress, eating, personality and sleep-wake disorders.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Distinguish GAD, panic disorder, agoraphobia, social anxiety and specific phobia, and name sertraline as NICE's first-choice drug for GAD.
  2. Outline OCD measurement with the Y-BOCS and treatment with exposure and response prevention and SSRIs.
  3. Distinguish ICD-11 PTSD, complex PTSD and adjustment disorder, and explain why single-session debriefing is not recommended.
  4. Distinguish bodily distress disorder, hypochondriasis, dissociative disorders, factitious disorder and malingering by intention and motive.
  5. Recognise anorexia nervosa, bulimia nervosa and binge eating disorder, and outline their NICE treatments and refeeding risks.
  6. Name sleep disorders from their timing in the night, such as night terrors in early NREM sleep, and offer CBT for insomnia first.
  7. Answer the chapter's 114 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
