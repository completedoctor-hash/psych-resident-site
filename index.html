Reference the College

How the exams are actually built

The Royal College of Psychiatrists sets and marks the MRCPsych. Both written papers run to 150 marks over three hours, and the College states they consist of multiple choice questions and extended matching items in a balance of approximately two thirds to one third.

Every section on this site carries the College weighting and the marks it is worth. Confirm current regulations with the College before you apply.
