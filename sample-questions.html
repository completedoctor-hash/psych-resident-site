Sample questions from the Paper A bank

Nine single best answer questions, one extended matching set and part of a revision note, exactly as they appear inside the bank. Nothing to sign in to and nothing to pay. Work them, then read the explanations.

    Single best answer

    Five options, one best answer. The explanation says why the answer is right and why the others are wrong.

    Extended matching

    One theme, eight options, two or three questions beneath it. An option may be used once, more than once, or not at all.

    The revision note

    Every topic closes with a note covering the concepts the questions have just tested, in short numbered points, ending with the major learning points. This is one block of one topic's note.

    What the full bank contains

    Every question is written for this bank against a published blueprint that follows the College mark weighting, so time spent here is time spent where the marks are. Each topic is checked mechanically before it is published: option counts, answer integrity, explanation depth, no repeated questions and an even spread of correct answers across A to E, so nobody learns to guess a letter.
