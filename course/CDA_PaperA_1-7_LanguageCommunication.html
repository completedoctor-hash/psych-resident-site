Aim and objectives

Aim. To explain the structure, development and functions of language and communication for MRCPsych Paper A.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Define phonemes, morphemes, syntax, semantics and pragmatics, with one example of each.
  2. Compare Chomsky's language acquisition device with learning and social interactionist accounts of language acquisition.
  3. Distinguish the strong and weak forms of the Sapir-Whorf hypothesis.
  4. Outline language milestones from cooing to sentences and the critical period hypothesis, including Lenneberg's work.
  5. Compare Piaget's and Vygotsky's views on language and thought, including private speech.
  6. Describe communicative competence and non-verbal cues, such as gaze, gesture, tone and personal space.
  7. Answer the chapter's 48 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
