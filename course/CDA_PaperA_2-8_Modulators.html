Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to explain how hormones, stress, inflammation and metabolism modulate brain function and mental illness for MRCPsych Paper A.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Describe the HPA axis (CRH, ACTH, cortisol), its negative feedback and the dexamethasone suppression test.
  2. Compare the fast sympathetic-adrenal-medullary and slower cortisol stress responses, and explain allostatic load.
  3. Recognise endocrine disorders that mimic mental illness, such as hypothyroidism and Cushing's syndrome.
  4. Explain sickness behaviour, raised inflammatory markers (IL-6, CRP) in depression, and anti-NMDA receptor encephalitis.
  5. Describe the roles of leptin and ghrelin, antipsychotic metabolic effects, pulsatile GnRH and hyperprolactinaemia.
  6. Answer the chapter's 100 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
