Aim and objectives

Aim. To explain the main theories of social cognition and their clinical relevance for MRCPsych Paper A.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Describe the affective, behavioural and cognitive components of attitudes, and measurement with Likert, Thurstone and semantic differential scales.
  2. Explain Festinger's cognitive dissonance and the elaboration likelihood model of persuasion.
  3. Describe Kelley's covariation model and biases such as the fundamental attribution error and actor-observer bias.
  4. Explain theory of mind and the Sally-Anne false-belief test in autism.
  5. Describe the components of the self, including self-concept and self-esteem, and disorders of the self such as depersonalisation.
  6. Answer the chapter's 64 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
