Aim and objectives

Aim. To explain sexual development, adolescence, adult life transitions and pregnancy for MRCPsych Paper A.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Distinguish gender identity, gender role and sexual orientation, and describe Kohlberg's stages of gender constancy.
  2. Describe Tanner staging of puberty and Erikson's adolescent stage of identity versus role confusion.
  3. Name Marcia's four identity statuses: achievement, moratorium, foreclosure and diffusion.
  4. Describe adult adaptations, such as pairing, parenting and midlife, and compare models of grief, including Bowlby's and Stroebe and Schut's.
  5. Describe the embryonic period as most sensitive to teratogens, and distinguish baby blues, postnatal depression and puerperal psychosis.
  6. Answer the chapter's 66 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
