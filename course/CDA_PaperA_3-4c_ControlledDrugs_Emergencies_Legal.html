Aim and objectives

Aim. To explain controlled drugs, emergency prescribing, adverse event reporting and the law in prescribing for MRCPsych Paper A.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Classify controlled drugs by Misuse of Drugs Act class and Misuse of Drugs Regulations schedule, with one example each.
  2. Compare acamprosate, naltrexone and disulfiram for alcohol, methadone and buprenorphine for opioids, and varenicline for smoking.
  3. Outline NICE rapid tranquillisation: de-escalation, oral medicine, then IM lorazepam or haloperidol with promethazine, and physical monitoring afterwards.
  4. Distinguish NMS, serotonin syndrome and akathisia by onset, signs and first-line management.
  5. Describe the MHRA Yellow Card scheme and the black triangle symbol.
  6. Describe legal aspects of prescribing, including consent under the Mental Health Act, DVLA rules and the valproate pregnancy prevention programme.
  7. Answer the chapter's 85 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
