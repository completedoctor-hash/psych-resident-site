Aim and objectives

Aim. To explain neuropeptides, endocytosis, opioid systems and the actions of recreational drugs for MRCPsych Paper A.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Distinguish neuromodulation from fast neurotransmission and describe how neuropeptides are made and released from dense-core vesicles.
  2. Name key neuropeptides, such as oxytocin, vasopressin, CRH, substance P and orexin, and one function of each.
  3. Describe clathrin-mediated endocytosis and its roles in vesicle recycling and receptor internalisation.
  4. Name the mu, delta and kappa opioid receptors and their endogenous ligands: beta-endorphin, enkephalins and dynorphin.
  5. Match recreational drugs to their main targets, such as cocaine at the dopamine transporter, cannabis at CB1 and LSD at 5-HT2A.
  6. Explain the shared role of mesolimbic dopamine release in the nucleus accumbens in drug reward.
  7. Answer the chapter's 68 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
