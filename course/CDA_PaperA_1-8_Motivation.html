Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to explain the psychological and biological theories of motivation, emotion and aggression for MRCPsych Paper A.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Compare Hull's drive reduction theory, the Yerkes-Dodson law and intrinsic versus extrinsic motivation, including the overjustification effect.
  2. Describe hypothalamic control of hunger and satiety and the roles of leptin and ghrelin in obesity.
  3. Compare the James-Lange, Cannon-Bard and Schachter-Singer theories of emotion.
  4. Explain aggression using the amygdala threat response, the frustration-aggression hypothesis and Bandura's Bobo doll studies.
  5. Explain how culture shapes motivation, using Baumeister and Leary's need to belong.
  6. Describe Maslow's hierarchy of needs and McClelland's need for achievement.
  7. Answer the chapter's 64 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
