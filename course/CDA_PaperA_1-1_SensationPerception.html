Aim and objectives

Aim. To explain the psychology of sensation, perception and attention for MRCPsych Paper A.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Define absolute and difference thresholds, and apply Weber's law and signal detection theory.
  2. Compare Gibson's direct theory of perception with Gregory's top-down constructivist theory.
  3. Describe the receptors and coding for hearing, balance, touch, pain (gate control theory), smell, taste and colour vision.
  4. Describe the dorsal and ventral visual streams, perceptual constancy and visual development.
  5. Name the Gestalt laws of perceptual organisation and describe figure-ground separation.
  6. Explain Broadbent's filter model of attention and the aberrant salience account of hallucinations.
  7. Answer the chapter's 105 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
