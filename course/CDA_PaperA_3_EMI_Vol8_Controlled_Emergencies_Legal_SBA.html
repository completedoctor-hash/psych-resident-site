Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare candidates for MRCPsych Paper A EMI questions on controlled drugs, emergencies, adverse event reporting and the law.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Assign named drugs to their Misuse of Drugs Act class and Regulations schedule.
  2. Match medicines for alcohol, opioid and nicotine dependence to their mechanism and main indication.
  3. Apply NICE rapid tranquillisation steps and PRN principles to an agitated patient in a vignette.
  4. Recognise NMS, serotonin syndrome and akathisia, and name the first step in managing each.
  5. Describe who can submit a Yellow Card report and which reactions to report for black triangle drugs.
  6. Apply legal rules on consent to treatment, driving and prescribing in pregnancy to a vignette.
  7. Answer the chapter's 67 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
