Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to explain theories of consciousness, sleep, altered states and the cognitive unconscious for MRCPsych Paper A.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Compare global workspace theory with higher-order theories of consciousness, and define neural correlates of consciousness.
  2. Distinguish dualism, identity theory and functionalism as positions on the mind-body problem.
  3. Describe NREM and REM sleep stages, the suprachiasmatic nucleus and melatonin, and Borbély's two-process model of sleep.
  4. Define altered states of consciousness and describe hypnosis, meditation and trance as examples.
  5. Explain the cognitive unconscious and Nisbett and Wilson's evidence that introspective reports are often confabulated.
  6. Distinguish arousal from awareness, and compare coma, vegetative state, minimally conscious state, locked-in syndrome and delirium.
  7. Answer the chapter's 80 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
