Aim and objectives

Aim. To build rapid recall of neuroanatomy, neural circuits, hormones and inflammatory responses so you can answer MRCPsych Paper A extended matching items accurately.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Localise lesions from deficits in the cranial nerves, basal ganglia, temporal lobe, frontal lobes and white matter tracts.
  2. Name the sources of ascending systems: locus coeruleus, raphe nuclei, ventral tegmental area, substantia nigra and nucleus basalis of Meynert.
  3. Describe circuits for reward, habit, memory, emotion and attention, and link them to OCD, ADHD and dementia.
  4. Localise the aphasias and agnosias, and name the default mode, salience and central executive networks.
  5. Describe the HPA axis and its negative feedback, and the thyroid and gonadal hormone axes in psychiatric illness.
  6. Explain inflammation's proposed role in depression, and recognise anti-NMDA receptor encephalitis as an autoimmune cause of psychosis.
  7. Answer the chapter's 101 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
