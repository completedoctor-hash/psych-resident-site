Aim and objectives

Aim. To explain the general principles of psychopharmacology, drug development and licensing for MRCPsych Paper A.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Describe classification of psychotropic drugs by indication, chemical structure and mechanism, including Neuroscience-based Nomenclature.
  2. Explain the placebo and nocebo effects and why placebo-controlled trials are needed.
  3. Distinguish compliance, adherence and concordance, and list reasons why about half of people do not take long-term medicines as prescribed.
  4. Name rating scales for efficacy and side effects, such as AIMS and the Barnes Akathisia Rating Scale.
  5. Outline drug development from preclinical testing through phases I to IV, including key features of an RCT.
  6. Describe MHRA licensing, the summary of product characteristics, off-label prescribing and generic bioequivalence.
  7. Answer the chapter's 72 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
