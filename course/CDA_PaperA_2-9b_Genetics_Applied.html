Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to explain genetic heterogeneity, epigenetics, gene editing, causal inference and clinical genetics for MRCPsych Paper A.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Define pleiotropy, genetic heterogeneity and endophenotypes, giving examples such as prepulse inhibition in schizophrenia.
  2. Describe DNA methylation and histone modification, and explain how early adversity can alter gene expression.
  3. Describe CRISPR-Cas9 gene editing, knockout models and induced pluripotent stem cells for testing genetic variants.
  4. Explain Mendelian randomisation and list its three core assumptions.
  5. Outline non-directive genetic counselling and explain why polygenic risk scores cannot yet predict psychiatric illness in individuals.
  6. Answer the chapter's 86 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
