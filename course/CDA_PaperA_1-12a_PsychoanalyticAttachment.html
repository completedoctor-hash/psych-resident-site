Aim and objectives

Aim. To explain psychoanalytic and attachment theories of development and the effects of childhood adversity for MRCPsych Paper A.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Outline Freud's psychosexual stages, Erikson's eight psychosocial stages and Klein's paranoid-schizoid and depressive positions.
  2. Describe Bowlby's attachment theory and the protest, despair and detachment response to separation.
  3. Classify Ainsworth's Strange Situation attachment types, adding Main's disorganised type.
  4. Distinguish privation from deprivation, drawing on Rutter's work.
  5. Describe how adverse childhood experiences, bereavement and parental divorce affect later mental health.
  6. Name protective factors that buffer children against adversity, such as a reliable caring adult.
  7. Answer the chapter's 100 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
