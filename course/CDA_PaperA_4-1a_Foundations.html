Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to apply the fundamental concepts of psychopathology, consciousness and cognition for MRCPsych Paper A.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Distinguish form from content and subjective from objective findings, and outline Jaspers' descriptive phenomenology.
  2. Describe levels of consciousness, from clouding to coma, and their effects on attention, thinking and memory.
  3. Distinguish anterograde and retrograde amnesia, confabulation and paramnesias such as deja vu and jamais vu.
  4. Explain how the pattern of cognitive failure points to the site of brain dysfunction.
  5. Answer the chapter's 20 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
