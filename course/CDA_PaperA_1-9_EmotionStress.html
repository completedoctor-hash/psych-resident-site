Aim and objectives

Aim. To explain the theories of emotion and stress and how people cope for MRCPsych Paper A.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Describe the components of emotion, Ekman's six basic emotions, and the survival, social and motivational functions of emotion.
  2. Compare the James-Lange, Cannon-Bard and Schachter-Singer two-factor theories of emotion.
  3. Describe the sympathetic-adrenal-medullary and HPA axis stress responses and Selye's general adaptation syndrome.
  4. Explain Holmes and Rahe's Social Readjustment Rating Scale, daily hassles and Type A behaviour.
  5. Distinguish problem-focused from emotion-focused coping (Lazarus and Folkman), and describe resilience and burnout.
  6. Answer the chapter's 81 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
