Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to explain, predict and manage the adverse effects of psychotropic drugs for MRCPsych Paper A.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Link receptor blockade to side effects: alpha-1 to postural hypotension, H1 to sedation and muscarinic M1 to anticholinergic effects.
  2. Explain hyperprolactinaemia from D2 blockade and outline the management of sexual side effects.
  3. Describe antipsychotic metabolic effects and the monitoring of weight, HbA1c and lipids.
  4. Recognise drug-induced QTc prolongation and state that a QTc over 500 ms needs urgent action.
  5. Distinguish acute dystonia, akathisia, parkinsonism and tardive dyskinesia by timing and treatment.
  6. Compare neuroleptic malignant syndrome and serotonin syndrome by onset, rigidity versus clonus, and treatment.
  7. Answer the chapter's 80 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
