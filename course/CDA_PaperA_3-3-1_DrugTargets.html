Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to explain the molecular targets of psychotropic drugs and how receptor binding predicts their effects for MRCPsych Paper A.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Name the main receptor subtypes for dopamine, serotonin, GABA and glutamate, and link each to a psychotropic action.
  2. Distinguish G protein-coupled receptors with seven transmembrane domains from ligand-gated ion channels such as the GABA-A receptor.
  3. Define affinity and occupancy, and state the 65 to 80% D2 occupancy window for antipsychotic effect.
  4. Use receptor binding profiles to predict side effects, such as sedation and weight gain from H1 blockade.
  5. Distinguish full agonists, partial agonists such as aripiprazole, antagonists and inverse agonists by intrinsic activity.
  6. Describe signalling cascades, pharmacodynamic interactions, and transporter targets including SERT, DAT, NET and VMAT2.
  7. Answer the chapter's 95 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
