Aim and objectives

Aim. To explain the principles, variation and methods of psychiatric genetics for MRCPsych Paper A.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Describe Mendelian inheritance patterns, and compare linkage with association studies.
  2. Explain heritability and how twin, adoption and family studies estimate it.
  3. Describe GWAS findings and polygenic risk scores for common psychiatric disorders.
  4. Describe rare copy number variants, such as the 22q11.2 deletion, and their large effect on psychiatric risk.
  5. Outline molecular methods, including PCR, sequencing, transcriptomics, proteomics and metabolomics.
  6. Answer the chapter's 85 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
