Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to explain the mechanisms of dementia drugs, hypnotics, cognitive enhancers and psychoactive substances for MRCPsych Paper A.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Compare donepezil, rivastigmine, galantamine and memantine by mechanism, and outline how anti-amyloid antibodies work.
  2. Compare hypnotics acting at GABA-A receptors (benzodiazepines, Z-drugs), melatonin receptor agonists and orexin receptor antagonists (DORAs).
  3. Explain how modafinil and stimulants act, distinguishing methylphenidate's transporter blockade from amphetamine's monoamine release.
  4. Compare methadone (full mu agonist) and buprenorphine (high-affinity partial agonist) in opioid substitution treatment.
  5. Explain THC's action at CB1 receptors, psychedelic 5-HT2A agonism, and MDMA-induced serotonin release with hyperthermia and hyponatraemia.
  6. Describe novel psychoactive substances, such as synthetic cannabinoids, and why they are more potent and harder to detect.
  7. Answer the chapter's 81 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
