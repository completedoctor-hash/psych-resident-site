Aim and objectives

Aim. To explain how life stage, interactions, brain entry, genes and drug levels shape psychotropic pharmacokinetics for MRCPsych Paper A.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Describe pharmacokinetic changes in children, pregnancy, older age and liver, kidney or heart disease.
  2. Explain CYP450 induction and inhibition, such as fluvoxamine raising and smoking lowering clozapine levels through CYP1A2.
  3. Explain how lipophilicity affects brain entry, volume of distribution and duration of action.
  4. Describe the blood-brain barrier and the role of P-glycoprotein efflux.
  5. Describe CYP2D6 and CYP2C19 metaboliser phenotypes and HLA-B*15:02 testing before carbamazepine.
  6. Apply therapeutic drug monitoring, including lithium levels taken 12 hours after the dose and clozapine levels.
  7. Answer the chapter's 80 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
