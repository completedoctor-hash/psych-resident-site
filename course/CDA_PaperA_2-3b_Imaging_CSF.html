Aim and objectives

Aim. To explain neuroimaging, microdialysis and CSF analysis as techniques in neuroscience for MRCPsych Paper A.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Compare CT, structural MRI and diffusion tensor imaging by what each shows about brain structure.
  2. Compare fMRI (BOLD signal), PET and SPECT, including DaTscan in dementia with Lewy bodies.
  3. Explain the principle of microdialysis and list its uses and limitations.
  4. Outline lumbar puncture indications and contraindications, including raised intracranial pressure.
  5. Interpret CSF findings in infection, Alzheimer's disease (low amyloid-beta 42, raised tau) and prion disease, and low 5-HIAA in violent suicide.
  6. Answer the chapter's 72 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
