Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to use physical examination and investigations in psychiatric assessment for MRCPsych Paper A.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Name physical illnesses that mimic psychiatric disorder, such as thyroid disease, and describe a focused physical examination.
  2. List baseline investigations before starting antipsychotics or lithium, including an ECG for the QTc interval.
  3. Describe the delta, theta, alpha and beta EEG rhythms and typical changes with drugs and disease.
  4. Compare structural (CT, MRI) and functional (PET, SPECT, fMRI) brain imaging.
  5. Describe the dexamethasone suppression test and other neuroendocrine tests.
  6. Answer the chapter's 42 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
