Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare candidates for MRCPsych Paper A EMI questions on classification, formulation, rating scales and risk assessment.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Compare ICD-11, DSM-5-TR and the ICF by what each classifies.
  2. Construct a biopsychosocial formulation using predisposing, precipitating, perpetuating and protective factors.
  3. Distinguish psychodynamic, cognitive-behavioural and systemic frameworks for explaining a presentation.
  4. Choose the right rating scale for diagnosis, caseness or change in a vignette.
  5. Describe collaborative safety planning and explain why NICE advises against using risk tools to predict suicide.
  6. Answer the chapter's 19 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
