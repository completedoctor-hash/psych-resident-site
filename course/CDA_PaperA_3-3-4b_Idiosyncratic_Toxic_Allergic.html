Aim and objectives

Aim. To explain idiosyncratic, toxic and allergic reactions to psychotropic drugs and their management for MRCPsych Paper A.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Distinguish dose-related (type A) from idiosyncratic (type B) adverse drug reactions, with two examples of each.
  2. Describe clozapine-induced neutropenia, agranulocytosis and myocarditis and the monitoring that detects them.
  3. Recognise Stevens-Johnson syndrome with carbamazepine and lamotrigine, and explain slow lamotrigine titration.
  4. Describe valproate hepatotoxicity and pancreatitis and the clinical signs of lithium toxicity.
  5. Outline immediate management of a serious drug reaction, including stopping the drug and Yellow Card reporting.
  6. Answer the chapter's 51 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
