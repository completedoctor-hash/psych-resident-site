Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to recognise and describe the psychopathology of self, speech, affect and movement for MRCPsych Paper A.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Describe Jaspers' four formal features of self-awareness, and distinguish depersonalisation, derealisation and passivity phenomena.
  2. Distinguish dysarthria and Broca's, Wernicke's and conduction aphasias from psychiatric speech disorders such as neologisms and pressure of speech.
  3. Distinguish mood from affect, and describe blunted, flat, labile and incongruous affect.
  4. Recognise catatonic signs, including waxy flexibility, mitgehen, echopraxia and negativism.
  5. Distinguish stereotypies, mannerisms and tics in a clinical vignette.
  6. Answer the chapter's 51 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
