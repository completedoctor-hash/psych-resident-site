Aim and objectives

Aim. To explain the theories, measurement and determinants of intelligence for MRCPsych Paper A.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Describe Spearman's general factor g and explain how factor analysis identifies it.
  2. Interpret IQ scores using a mean of 100 and standard deviation of 15, and describe the WAIS.
  3. Describe the Cattell-Horn-Carroll model and distinguish fluid from crystallised intelligence.
  4. Compare Sternberg's triarchic theory, Gardner's multiple intelligences and emotional intelligence.
  5. Explain the Flynn effect and cultural bias in intelligence testing.
  6. Describe twin and adoption evidence on heritability of IQ and explain why heritability rises with age.
  7. Answer the chapter's 56 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
