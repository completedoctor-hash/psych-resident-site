Aim and objectives

Aim. To build rapid recall of the mechanisms of action of the major psychotropic and psychoactive drug classes so you can answer MRCPsych Paper A extended matching items accurately.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Explain D2 blockade by antipsychotics, aripiprazole's partial agonism and how receptor profiles shape side effects.
  2. Describe mechanisms of SSRIs, SNRIs, tricyclics, MAOIs and mirtazapine, and proposed lithium mechanisms including GSK-3 inhibition.
  3. Describe mechanisms of benzodiazepines, Z-drugs, pregabalin, melatonin agonists and orexin antagonists.
  4. Describe cholinesterase inhibitors, memantine as an NMDA antagonist, anti-amyloid antibodies, and cognitive enhancers such as modafinil.
  5. Compare methadone (full mu agonist) with buprenorphine (partial agonist), and methylphenidate (reuptake blocker) with amphetamine (releaser).
  6. Explain how THC acts at CB1, psilocybin and LSD at 5-HT2A, MDMA through serotonin release, and why novel psychoactive substances evade drug screens.
  7. Answer the chapter's 85 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
