Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare candidates for MRCPsych Paper A EMI questions on intelligence, thought, language, motivation and emotion.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Compare Spearman's g, Cattell's fluid and crystallised intelligence, Gardner's multiple intelligences and Sternberg's triarchic theory.
  2. Interpret IQ scores (mean 100, standard deviation 15) and describe the Flynn effect.
  3. Describe executive functions, availability and representativeness heuristics and Festinger's cognitive dissonance.
  4. Compare Chomsky's language acquisition device with the Sapir-Whorf hypothesis.
  5. Describe Maslow's hierarchy, the Yerkes-Dodson law and hypothalamic control of hunger, including leptin and ghrelin.
  6. Compare James-Lange, Cannon-Bard and Schachter-Singer theories of emotion and Lazarus's appraisal model of stress.
  7. Answer the chapter's 76 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
