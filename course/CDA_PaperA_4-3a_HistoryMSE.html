Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to take a structured psychiatric history and perform and record a mental state examination for MRCPsych Paper A.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. List the components of a psychiatric history, from presenting complaint to personal and premorbid history.
  2. Describe interviewing skills, including moving from open to closed questions and summarising.
  3. List the headings of the mental state examination in order, from appearance and behaviour to insight.
  4. Distinguish mood from affect, and circumstantiality from tangentiality, in a clinical vignette.
  5. Record mental state findings in descriptive terms rather than diagnostic labels.
  6. Answer the chapter's 34 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
