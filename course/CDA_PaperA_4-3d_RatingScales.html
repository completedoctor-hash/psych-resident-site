Aim and objectives

Aim. To explain the types, uses and formats of rating scales in psychiatry for MRCPsych Paper A.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Name structured diagnostic instruments, such as the SCID, SCAN and CIDI, and state what each produces.
  2. Describe caseness scales, such as the GHQ and PHQ-9, and how the cut-off trades false positives against false negatives.
  3. Name scales built to measure change, such as the HAM-D, MADRS, PANSS and Y-BOCS.
  4. Compare self-rated and clinician-rated scales, and structured and semi-structured interviews.
  5. Distinguish Likert and visual analogue scales and the type of data each produces.
  6. Answer the chapter's 50 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
