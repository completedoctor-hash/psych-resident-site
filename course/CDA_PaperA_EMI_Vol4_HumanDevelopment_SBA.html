Aim and objectives

Aim. To build rapid recall of the concepts and theories of human development across the lifespan so you can answer MRCPsych Paper A extended matching items accurately.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Compare cross-sectional and longitudinal designs, and describe gene-environment interaction using Caspi's 5-HTTLPR study.
  2. Outline Freud's psychosexual stages and Erikson's eight psychosocial stages.
  3. Describe Bowlby's attachment theory and the four Strange Situation categories: secure, avoidant, ambivalent and disorganised.
  4. Explain the effects of childhood adversity, loss and divorce, including Rutter's work on deprivation and recovery.
  5. Describe Baumrind's parenting styles, Thomas and Chess's temperament types and Kohlberg's stages of moral development.
  6. Describe Marcia's four identity statuses, normal grief, and psychological changes in later life.
  7. Answer the chapter's 98 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
