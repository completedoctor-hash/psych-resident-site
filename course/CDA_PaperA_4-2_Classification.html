Aim and objectives

Aim. To explain the main classificatory systems used in psychiatry and in disability for MRCPsych Paper A.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Compare ICD-11 (WHO) and DSM-5-TR (APA) by purpose, structure and use.
  2. Describe key ICD-11 changes, such as removal of schizophrenia subtypes and the dimensional model of personality disorder.
  3. Explain the strengths and weaknesses of categorical and dimensional classification.
  4. Describe the ICF components: body functions and structures, activities, participation, and environmental and personal factors.
  5. Distinguish intellectual disability from a specific learning difficulty such as dyslexia, and describe Education, Health and Care plans.
  6. Answer the chapter's 55 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
