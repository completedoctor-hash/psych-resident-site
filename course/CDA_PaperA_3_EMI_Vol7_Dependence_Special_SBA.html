Aim and objectives

Aim. To build rapid recall of dependence, withdrawal and prescribing in special circumstances so you can answer MRCPsych Paper A extended matching items accurately.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Explain the neuroadaptation underlying tolerance and withdrawal for alcohol, opioids and benzodiazepines.
  2. Distinguish antidepressant discontinuation symptoms from relapse and plan a gradual taper.
  3. Select medicines for children and adolescents, such as fluoxetine for depression and methylphenidate for ADHD, with growth monitoring.
  4. Adjust prescribing for older adults using STOPP/START criteria, avoiding anticholinergic drugs and benzodiazepines where possible.
  5. Choose safer psychotropics in liver, kidney and heart disease and in eating disorders.
  6. Apply prescribing principles in pregnancy and breastfeeding, including avoiding valproate, and treat PMDD and menopausal mood symptoms.
  7. Answer the chapter's 84 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
