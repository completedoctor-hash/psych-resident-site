Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to construct formulations using biopsychosocial, psychodynamic, cognitive-behavioural and systemic frameworks for MRCPsych Paper A.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Build a biopsychosocial formulation grid crossing the four Ps with biological, psychological and social factors in a vignette.
  2. Describe Freud's structural model and rank common defence mechanisms by Vaillant's levels of maturity.
  3. Apply Beck's cognitive model, including the cognitive triad, core beliefs and assumptions, to a five-areas formulation.
  4. Describe systemic concepts, including circular causality, the family life cycle and Minuchin's structural family therapy.
  5. Compare the strengths and limits of each framework for a given patient.
  6. Answer the chapter's 59 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
