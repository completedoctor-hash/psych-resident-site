Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to describe and classify disorders of perception, thought form and belief for MRCPsych Paper A.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Distinguish illusions, hallucinations and pseudohallucinations, and name special types such as functional and reflex hallucinations.
  2. Recognise Schneider's first-rank auditory hallucinations: thought echo, third-person voices and running commentary.
  3. Distinguish types of formal thought disorder, such as derailment, tangentiality, circumstantiality and flight of ideas.
  4. Define a delusion using Jaspers' criteria and distinguish primary from secondary delusions.
  5. Distinguish delusions from overvalued ideas and obsessions.
  6. Name delusional misidentification and other syndromes, such as Capgras, Fregoli, Cotard and de Clérambault.
  7. Answer the chapter's 83 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
