Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to assess risk and build collaborative safety plans for MRCPsych Paper A.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Explain why risk tools and scales should not be used to predict suicide or decide who receives care, as advised by NICE.
  2. Distinguish static from dynamic risk factors and give two examples of each.
  3. Describe the parts of a collaborative safety plan, including warning signs, coping strategies, contacts and means restriction.
  4. Formulate risk in a clinical vignette and link it to a management plan.
  5. Answer the chapter's 7 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
