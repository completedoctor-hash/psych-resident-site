Aim and objectives

Aim. To explain the neurobiological basis of drug, physical and psychological treatments for MRCPsych Paper A.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Explain how ketamine and esketamine act at NMDA receptors to produce rapid antidepressant effects through synaptogenesis.
  2. Describe CYP2D6 and CYP2C19 metaboliser status and the HLA-B*15:02 risk of Stevens-Johnson syndrome with carbamazepine.
  3. Explain proposed mechanisms of ECT, including increased BDNF and hippocampal neurogenesis, and evidence that psychotherapy changes brain function.
  4. Compare rTMS, tDCS, deep brain stimulation and vagus nerve stimulation by mechanism and main indication.
  5. Describe sympathetic and parasympathetic functions and transmitters, and link anticholinergic drug effects to them.
  6. Answer the chapter's 85 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
