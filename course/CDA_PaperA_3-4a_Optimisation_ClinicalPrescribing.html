Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to optimise medicines, prescribe rationally and monitor treatment for MRCPsych Paper A.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Describe how to agree shared, measurable treatment goals before starting a medicine.
  2. Explain how diagnosis, severity and phase of illness (acute, continuation, maintenance) guide drug choice.
  3. Adjust prescribing for physical illness, interacting medicines, children and older adults.
  4. Describe minimum effective doses, maximum doses and the length of an adequate trial.
  5. Outline a stepwise approach to poor response, and define treatment resistance after two adequate trials.
  6. Plan safe switching and stopping, and monitor efficacy and adverse effects on a set timetable.
  7. Answer the chapter's 94 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
