Aim and objectives

Aim. To explain the mechanisms of action of antipsychotics, antidepressants, mood stabilisers and anxiolytics for MRCPsych Paper A.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Explain D2 receptor blockade, including the 60 to 80% occupancy window and higher risk of extrapyramidal effects above 80%.
  2. Compare receptor profiles of clozapine, aripiprazole (D2 partial agonist) and amisulpride.
  3. Describe mechanisms of SSRIs, SNRIs, tricyclics, MAOIs and mirtazapine (alpha-2 antagonist).
  4. Outline proposed mechanisms of lithium, including inositol depletion and GSK-3 inhibition, and of valproate and lamotrigine.
  5. Describe mechanisms of benzodiazepines, buspirone (5-HT1A partial agonist), pregabalin (alpha-2-delta calcium channel) and propranolol.
  6. Answer the chapter's 117 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
