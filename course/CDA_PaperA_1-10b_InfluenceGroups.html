Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to explain the main theories of social influence, group processes, aggression and attraction for MRCPsych Paper A.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Name French and Raven's five bases of social power and match each to a ward example, and compare Lewin's three leadership styles.
  2. Describe Asch's conformity and Milgram's obedience studies and list Janis's features of groupthink.
  3. Describe self-disclosure and Altman and Taylor's social penetration theory of developing relationships.
  4. Explain Zajonc's social facilitation, social loafing, Tajfel's social identity theory and Allport's contact hypothesis for reducing prejudice.
  5. Compare the frustration-aggression hypothesis with Bandura's Bobo doll studies of social learning.
  6. Explain Latané and Darley's bystander effect, equity theory, and attraction through proximity, similarity and reciprocity.
  7. Answer the chapter's 122 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
