Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to explain brain metabolism, neurotransmitter synthesis and synaptic transmission for MRCPsych Paper A.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Describe the brain's high glucose and oxygen use (about 20% of resting oxygen) and thiamine's role as an enzyme cofactor.
  2. Outline synthesis of dopamine from tyrosine and serotonin from tryptophan, naming the rate-limiting hydroxylase enzymes.
  3. Name the breakdown enzymes MAO, COMT and acetylcholinesterase, and the metabolites HVA and 5-HIAA.
  4. Compare chemical and electrical (gap junction) synapses, and describe calcium-triggered vesicle release via SNARE proteins and reuptake.
  5. Distinguish ionotropic receptors such as GABA-A and NMDA from metabotropic receptors such as dopamine D2.
  6. Explain second messenger pathways: cAMP via Gs and Gi proteins, and IP3 and DAG via Gq.
  7. Answer the chapter's 94 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
