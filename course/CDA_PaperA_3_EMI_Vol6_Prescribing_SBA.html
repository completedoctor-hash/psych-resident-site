Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you for MRCPsych Paper A questions on medicine optimisation, clinical prescribing and monitoring.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Describe how treatment goals, diagnosis, severity and illness phase shape the choice of medicine.
  2. Adjust prescribing for physical illness, interacting medicines, children and older adults in a clinical vignette.
  3. Weigh benefit against harm for an individual, including pregnancy plans and legal safeguards.
  4. Describe minimum effective doses, maximum doses and adequate trial lengths, and a stepwise approach to poor response.
  5. Define treatment resistance and plan safe switching and stopping to avoid interactions and discontinuation symptoms.
  6. Outline a monitoring timetable for efficacy and tolerability.
  7. Answer the chapter's 101 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
