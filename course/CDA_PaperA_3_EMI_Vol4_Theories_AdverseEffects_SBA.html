Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare candidates for MRCPsych Paper A EMI questions on pharmacological theories of illness and adverse drug effects.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Explain the dopamine hypothesis of psychosis and the glutamate NMDA receptor hypofunction hypothesis.
  2. Describe Schildkraut's biogenic amine hypothesis of mood disorders and its main limitations.
  3. Outline the cholinergic hypothesis of Alzheimer's disease, Janowsky's cholinergic-adrenergic balance hypothesis and the amyloid cascade hypothesis.
  4. Predict side effects from receptor blockade: H1 sedation and weight gain, muscarinic anticholinergic effects and alpha-1 postural hypotension.
  5. Distinguish acute dystonia, akathisia, parkinsonism, tardive dyskinesia, NMS and serotonin syndrome by timing and signs.
  6. Recognise idiosyncratic, toxic and allergic reactions, such as agranulocytosis and Stevens-Johnson syndrome, in a vignette.
  7. Answer the chapter's 98 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
