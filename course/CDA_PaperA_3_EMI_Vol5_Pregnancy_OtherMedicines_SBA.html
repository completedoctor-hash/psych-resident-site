Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to answer extended matching questions on drugs in pregnancy, other medications, physical treatments and herbal remedies for MRCPsych Paper A.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Recognise the teratogenic risks of valproate, lithium and carbamazepine, and choose safer options in pregnancy and breastfeeding.
  2. Identify interactions between psychotropics and antiepileptics, analgesics and other medicines in a clinical vignette.
  3. Describe uses of antimuscarinics, beta-blockers, dopamine agonists and adrenergic agonists in psychiatric practice.
  4. Outline the putative neurochemical changes produced by ECT, rTMS and vagus nerve stimulation.
  5. Name the interactions and risks of herbal remedies such as St John's wort and ginkgo biloba.
  6. Answer the chapter's 81 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
