Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to answer extended matching questions on neuropsychology, assessment, neuroscience techniques, cells and neurochemistry for MRCPsych Paper A.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Explain double dissociation and how brain injury reveals the separate parts of cognition.
  2. Match cognitive tests to domains, such as the Wisconsin Card Sorting Test for set-shifting and the Stroop test for inhibition.
  3. Compare single-unit recording, EEG rhythms and structural and functional imaging, including CT, MRI, PET and fMRI.
  4. Describe neurone and glial types, the six layers of the neocortex, and the action potential.
  5. Describe synthesis of dopamine from tyrosine (rate-limited by tyrosine hydroxylase) and serotonin from tryptophan.
  6. Answer the chapter's 148 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
