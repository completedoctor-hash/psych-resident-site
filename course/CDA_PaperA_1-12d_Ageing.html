Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to describe normal ageing and its effects on physical, social, cognitive and emotional functioning for MRCPsych Paper A.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Distinguish age-related decline in processing speed and fluid intelligence from preserved crystallised intelligence.
  2. Describe common physical and sensory changes of ageing, including presbyopia and presbycusis.
  3. Describe sleep changes in later life, including earlier timing, less slow-wave sleep and more night waking.
  4. Compare disengagement theory, activity theory and Carstensen's socioemotional selectivity theory of social ageing.
  5. Explain why emotional wellbeing is often maintained in later life, with reference to the positivity effect.
  6. Answer the chapter's 34 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
