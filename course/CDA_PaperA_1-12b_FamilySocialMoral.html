Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to explain theories of family, temperament, social, moral and emotional development in childhood for MRCPsych Paper A.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Describe Baumrind's authoritative, authoritarian and permissive parenting styles, Maccoby and Martin's neglectful style, and their outcomes.
  2. Name Thomas and Chess's three temperament types (easy, difficult, slow-to-warm-up) and list factors that promote resilience.
  3. Outline Parten's stages of play and the peer status groups: popular, rejected, neglected, controversial and average.
  4. Describe Kohlberg's three levels and six stages of moral reasoning and Gilligan's ethic-of-care critique.
  5. Outline the order of childhood fears, from loud noises and strangers in infancy to failure and death in adolescence, and how emotion regulation develops.
  6. Answer the chapter's 72 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
