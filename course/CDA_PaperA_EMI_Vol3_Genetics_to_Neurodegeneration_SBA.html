Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to answer extended matching questions on genetics, neurodevelopment, syndromes, treatments and neurodegeneration for MRCPsych Paper A.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Describe patterns of inheritance, GWAS and polygenic risk, and rare variants such as the 22q11.2 deletion.
  2. Explain the neurodevelopmental hypothesis of schizophrenia and the effect of early adversity on the stress system.
  3. Describe the integrated neurobiology of autism, ADHD, addiction and anxiety disorders.
  4. Explain rapid-acting treatments such as ketamine, pharmacogenomic risks such as HLA-B*1502, and the neurobiology of ECT.
  5. Distinguish Alzheimer's disease, vascular dementia, Lewy body dementia (alpha-synuclein) and frontotemporal dementia by pathology.
  6. Answer the chapter's 150 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
