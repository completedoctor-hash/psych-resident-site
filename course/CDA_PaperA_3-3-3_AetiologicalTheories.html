Aim and objectives

Aim. To explain the major aetiological theories of psychiatric disorder that rest on pharmacological evidence for MRCPsych Paper A.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Explain the dopamine hypothesis of psychosis, including Howes and Kapur's version III.
  2. Describe the monoamine hypothesis of depression and the supporting evidence from reserpine and antidepressants.
  3. Explain the cholinergic hypothesis of memory loss in Alzheimer's disease and loss of the nucleus basalis of Meynert.
  4. Describe Janowsky's cholinergic-adrenergic balance hypothesis of mood.
  5. Explain NMDA receptor hypofunction in psychosis using evidence from ketamine and phencyclidine.
  6. Describe the amyloid cascade hypothesis and supporting evidence from APP mutations and Down syndrome.
  7. Answer the chapter's 60 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
