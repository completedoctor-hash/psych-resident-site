Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you for MRCPsych Paper A questions on receptors, channels, transporters and enzymes targeted by psychotropic drugs.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Name the main receptor subtypes for dopamine, serotonin, GABA and glutamate.
  2. Distinguish G protein-coupled receptors from ligand-gated ion channels, with an example of each.
  3. Define affinity and occupancy, and state the roughly 65 to 80% D2 occupancy range for antipsychotic effect.
  4. Relate receptor binding profiles to side effects, such as H1 blockade causing sedation and M1 blockade causing anticholinergic effects.
  5. Distinguish full agonists, partial agonists such as aripiprazole, antagonists and inverse agonists.
  6. Describe signalling cascades, pharmacodynamic interactions, and SERT, DAT and VMAT transporters.
  7. Answer the chapter's 80 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
