Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to explain the neural circuits controlling appetite, sleep, sex, aggression, pain and movement for MRCPsych Paper A.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Describe hypothalamic control of appetite, including the arcuate nucleus, leptin and ghrelin, and how medicines disrupt it.
  2. Explain the flip-flop switch between the ventrolateral preoptic nucleus and arousal systems, and the role of the suprachiasmatic nucleus.
  3. Describe the hormonal and neurotransmitter control of sexual function, including how raised prolactin from antipsychotics impairs it.
  4. Distinguish impulsive from instrumental aggression, linking impulsive aggression to amygdala overactivity and weak prefrontal and serotonergic control.
  5. Explain Melzack and Wall's gate control theory and descending pain modulation from the periaqueductal grey.
  6. Describe the direct and indirect basal ganglia pathways and link nigrostriatal D2 blockade to extrapyramidal side effects.
  7. Answer the chapter's 103 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
