Aim and objectives

Aim. To explain the social science and socio-cultural concepts that shape mental illness and its care for MRCPsych Paper A.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Describe the link between social class and mental illness, contrasting social causation with social drift.
  2. Describe Parsons' sick role and Mechanic's concept of illness behaviour.
  3. Explain expressed emotion in relapse of schizophrenia and Brown and Harris's vulnerability factors for depression in women.
  4. Describe Goffman's total institution and the features of institutionalisation.
  5. Define stigma and explain raised rates of psychosis in migrants and some ethnic minority groups.
  6. Describe Berry's acculturation strategies and pathoplasticity, and apply a cultural formulation to a vignette.
  7. Answer the chapter's 200 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
