Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to explain the pharmacokinetic factors that determine bioavailability of psychotropic drugs for MRCPsych Paper A.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Describe absorption, distribution, metabolism and excretion (ADME), and how a second drug can alter each.
  2. Compare oral, sublingual, transdermal, intranasal and intramuscular routes, and explain first-pass metabolism.
  3. Explain why only the free fraction of a protein-bound drug is active, and how hypoalbuminaemia changes it.
  4. Define half-life, volume of distribution, Cmax, Tmax and AUC, and state that steady state takes about four to five half-lives.
  5. Distinguish first-order from zero-order kinetics, giving alcohol and high-dose phenytoin as zero-order examples.
  6. Describe CYP450 interactions, such as smoking inducing CYP1A2 and lowering clozapine and olanzapine levels.
  7. Answer the chapter's 92 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
