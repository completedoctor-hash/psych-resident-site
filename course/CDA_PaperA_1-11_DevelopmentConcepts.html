Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to explain the theories, influences and research methods underlying human development for MRCPsych Paper A.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Compare Piaget's cognitive stages, Erikson's eight psychosocial stages and Freud's psychosexual stages.
  2. Explain how the timing and duration of adversity shape outcomes, using Rutter's English and Romanian Adoptees study.
  3. Distinguish cross-sectional, longitudinal and cohort-sequential designs, and state one strength and one weakness of each.
  4. Describe passive, evocative and active gene-environment correlation, and explain epigenetic change such as DNA methylation.
  5. Describe brain development on neuroimaging, including adolescent synaptic pruning and late prefrontal maturation.
  6. Answer the chapter's 51 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
