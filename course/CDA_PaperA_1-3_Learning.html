Aim and objectives

Aim. To explain the main theories of learning and their clinical uses for MRCPsych Paper A.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Describe the neural basis of learning, including long-term potentiation, and Seligman's concept of preparedness.
  2. Explain Pavlov's classical conditioning, including acquisition, extinction and spontaneous recovery, using Watson's Little Albert study.
  3. Distinguish positive and negative reinforcement from punishment, using Thorndike's law of effect and Skinner's operant conditioning.
  4. Compare fixed and variable ratio and interval schedules, and identify variable ratio as the most resistant to extinction.
  5. Describe Bandura's observational learning, Tolman's latent learning, Vygotsky's zone of proximal development and Bruner's scaffolding.
  6. Apply learning theory to clinical problems, including Mowrer's two-factor theory, systematic desensitisation and learned helplessness.
  7. Answer the chapter's 115 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
