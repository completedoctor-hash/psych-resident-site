Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you for MRCPsych Paper A questions on social cognition, influence, group processes, society and culture.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Describe attitude models, attribution biases and theory of mind.
  2. Name French and Raven's five bases of power, and describe Asch's conformity and Milgram's obedience studies.
  3. Explain social facilitation, social loafing, groupthink and Allport's contact hypothesis, and compare theories of aggression.
  4. Describe Latané and Darley's bystander effect and the main factors in interpersonal attraction.
  5. Describe social class gradients, Parsons' sick role, expressed emotion and Goffman's account of institutionalisation.
  6. Explain stigma, ethnic differences in psychosis, Berry's acculturation strategies and Beauchamp and Childress's four ethical principles.
  7. Answer the chapter's 163 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
