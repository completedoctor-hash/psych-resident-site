Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to answer extended matching questions on general pharmacology and pharmacokinetics for MRCPsych Paper A.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Classify psychotropics by indication, chemical structure and Neuroscience-based Nomenclature (NbN).
  2. Explain placebo and nocebo effects, and describe the causes of non-adherence and ways to improve it.
  3. Describe ADME, routes of administration, first-pass metabolism and protein binding.
  4. Define half-life, steady state and first- versus zero-order kinetics, and apply them to dosing.
  5. Explain how age, pregnancy and organ disease alter drug handling, and how lipid solubility affects brain entry.
  6. Describe pharmacogenomics, such as CYP2D6 poor metabolisers and HLA-B*1502 with carbamazepine, and therapeutic drug monitoring such as lithium levels.
  7. Answer the chapter's 100 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
