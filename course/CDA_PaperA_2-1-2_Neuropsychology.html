Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to explain core neuropsychological concepts and select appropriate cognitive tests for MRCPsych Paper A.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Explain single and double dissociation and how patients such as HM inform models of normal cognition.
  2. Compare localisation, modularity and distributed network accounts of cognitive function.
  3. Match tests to domains: WAIS-IV for IQ, NART for premorbid IQ, Wisconsin Card Sorting for set shifting, Rey-Osterrieth figure for visuospatial memory.
  4. Compare cognitive screening tools: MMSE (out of 30), MoCA (out of 30) and ACE-III (out of 100).
  5. Explain reliability, validity and floor and ceiling effects when interpreting cognitive test scores.
  6. Answer the chapter's 73 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
