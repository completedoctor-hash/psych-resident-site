Help and getting started

Everything a new member needs, in four steps.

Getting started

  1. Register on the register page with your email and a password.
  2. Confirm your email. We send a link from no-reply@netlify.com. Check your spam or junk folder if it has not arrived within 5 minutes.
  3. Sign in, then buy your course on the prices page. Pay by card on Stripe's secure page.
  4. Access opens straight away. You return to the site and your course is unlocked. If a chapter still says it is locked, sign out and sign back in.

Paid but still locked?

Make sure you are signed in with the same email you paid with. Then sign out and in again. If it is still locked, email completedoctor@gmail.com with your payment receipt and we will open it by hand, usually within a few hours.

Forgot your password?

Use "Forgot password" on the My account page. The reset email may go to spam.

Which devices?

Any up-to-date browser on a phone, tablet or computer. Your progress on flashcards is saved on each device separately.
