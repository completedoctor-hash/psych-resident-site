Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to describe and measure disease in populations, including mortality, standardisation and prevention, for MRCPsych Paper B.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Define endemic, hyperendemic, sporadic, epidemic and pandemic patterns of disease.
  2. Calculate incidence and prevalence from a worked example and explain how illness duration links them.
  3. Calculate crude, age-specific and age-adjusted death rates, and the standardised mortality ratio as observed over expected deaths.
  4. Compare direct and indirect standardisation and state when each is preferred.
  5. Distinguish primary, secondary and tertiary prevention, and describe Goldberg and Huxley's five levels and four filters model.
  6. Answer the chapter's 41 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
