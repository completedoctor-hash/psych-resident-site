Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to appraise sampling, validity and reliability in research for MRCPsych Paper B.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Distinguish a sample from a population, and compare simple random, stratified, cluster, systematic and convenience sampling.
  2. Define face, content, construct (convergent, divergent) and criterion (concurrent, predictive) validity.
  3. Distinguish test-retest, inter-rater, parallel-form and split-half reliability.
  4. Interpret Cohen's kappa as agreement beyond chance.
  5. Distinguish random from systematic error and their effects on precision and accuracy.
  6. Answer the chapter's 44 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
