Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare candidates to appraise the design, conduct and analysis of experimental studies and trials for MRCPsych Paper B.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Compare parallel, crossover, factorial, cluster and non-inferiority trial designs, naming one strength and one weakness of each.
  2. Distinguish simple, block and stratified randomisation and minimisation, and explain allocation concealment.
  3. Explain how blinding of participants, clinicians and assessors prevents performance and detection bias.
  4. Compare intention-to-treat and per-protocol analysis, and methods for missing data such as last observation carried forward.
  5. Distinguish internal from external validity, primary from surrogate endpoints, and trial phases I to IV.
  6. Answer the chapter's 52 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
