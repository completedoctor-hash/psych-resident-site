Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to critically appraise systematic reviews, meta-analyses and network meta-analyses for MRCPsych Paper B.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Distinguish primary research, secondary research and meta-analysis.
  2. Describe searching electronic databases, screening and the PRISMA 2020 flow diagram and 27-item checklist.
  3. Interpret heterogeneity (I squared), study weighting and a forest plot, and compare fixed-effect and random-effects models.
  4. Recognise publication bias on a funnel plot and name quality tools such as the Cochrane risk of bias tool and AMSTAR 2.
  5. Explain how network meta-analysis uses indirect comparisons and why it depends on the transitivity assumption.
  6. Answer the chapter's 38 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
