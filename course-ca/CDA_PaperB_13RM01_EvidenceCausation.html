Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare candidates to find evidence, frame clinical questions and argue causation for MRCPsych Paper B critical appraisal.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Describe the hierarchy of evidence and explain why the best design depends on the type of question.
  2. Frame a clinical question using PICO and outline a reproducible literature search.
  3. Calculate absolute risk reduction and number needed to treat, and express risk as natural frequencies for shared decisions.
  4. List the Bradford Hill criteria for causation and apply them to an observed association.
  5. Explain chance, bias, confounding and reverse causation as alternative explanations for an association.
  6. Answer the chapter's 29 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
