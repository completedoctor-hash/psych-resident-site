Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to describe and appraise descriptive and analytical observational designs for MRCPsych Paper B.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Describe the uses and limits of case reports and case series.
  2. Explain how cross-sectional surveys measure prevalence at a single time point.
  3. Explain the ecological fallacy in an ecological or correlational study.
  4. Describe the case-control design, control selection, and why it gives odds ratios but not incidence.
  5. Describe the cohort design and calculate incidence and relative risk.
  6. Explain how the self-controlled case series cancels fixed confounders by comparing each case with themselves.
  7. Answer the chapter's 44 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
