Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to apply clinical governance, quality improvement, reporting guidelines, network analysis, research ethics and AI principles for MRCPsych Paper B.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Distinguish clinical audit, service evaluation and research, and describe the stages of the audit cycle.
  2. Describe Plan-Do-Study-Act cycles and interpret run charts and statistical process control charts.
  3. Match reporting guidelines to designs: CONSORT for trials, STROBE for observational studies, PRISMA for reviews and STARD for diagnostic accuracy.
  4. Explain network analysis of symptoms, including nodes, edges and centrality.
  5. Outline research ethics principles, including informed consent, ethics committee approval and research with adults lacking capacity under the Mental Capacity Act.
  6. Describe the risks of AI in research, including biased training data, fabricated outputs and lack of accountability.
  7. Answer the chapter's 56 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
