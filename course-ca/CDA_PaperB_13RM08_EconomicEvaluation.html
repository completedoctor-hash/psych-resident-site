Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare you to compare, calculate and appraise types of health economic evaluation for MRCPsych Paper B.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Compare cost-minimisation, cost-benefit, cost-effectiveness, cost-utility and cost-consequence analyses by how outcomes are measured.
  2. Calculate QALYs from years lived and utility, describe time trade-off and standard gamble, and distinguish QALYs from DALYs.
  3. Calculate an ICER and interpret it on the cost-effectiveness plane and acceptability curve against NICE's willingness-to-pay threshold.
  4. Distinguish direct, indirect and intangible costs, define opportunity cost, and explain discounting at NICE's 3.5% per year.
  5. Appraise an economic evaluation and distinguish one-way, multi-way and probabilistic sensitivity analysis.
  6. Answer the chapter's 49 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
