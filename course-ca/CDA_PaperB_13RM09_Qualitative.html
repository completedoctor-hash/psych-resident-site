Aim and objectives

Aim. To prepare candidates to interpret and appraise qualitative research and attitude scales for MRCPsych Paper B.

At the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

  1. Explain when qualitative or mixed methods are the right choice for a research question.
  2. Distinguish grounded theory, phenomenology, ethnography and action research by the question each asks.
  3. Describe purposive, snowball and theoretical sampling, and define data saturation.
  4. Compare in-depth interviews, focus groups and participant observation for collecting qualitative data.
  5. Describe thematic analysis and rigour checks, including triangulation, respondent validation and reflexivity.
  6. Distinguish Likert, Thurstone, Guttman and visual analogue scales by how each turns attitudes into numbers.
  7. Answer the chapter's 55 exam-style questions, scoring at least 80%, by the end of this chapter.
