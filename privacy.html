Privacy notice

How we collect and use your personal data. Last updated 6 November 2026.

Who controls your data

The Complete Doctor Academy (The Psychiatric Resident) is the data controller. Contact: completedoctor@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Your email address and password (stored securely by our sign-in provider, Netlify Identity; we never see your password).
  • The courses you buy and when your access ends.
  • Payment details are handled entirely by Stripe. We receive your email, the amount and the course, never your card number.
  • Study progress, such as flashcards seen, is stored only in your own browser on your device.

Why we use it (lawful basis)

To provide the courses you buy (contract), to keep the service secure and prevent misuse (legitimate interests), and to keep payment records (legal obligation).

Who we share it with

Netlify (hosting and sign-in) and Stripe (payments), who act as our processors. Some processing may take place outside the UK under appropriate safeguards. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

Account data is kept while your account is open and for up to 2 years after your last access. Payment records are kept for 6 years, as required for tax.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or object to its use, by emailing us. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Cookies

We use only essential storage needed to keep you signed in and remember your progress. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.
