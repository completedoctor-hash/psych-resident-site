Wednesday 14:00 · Perinatal mental health clinic

The task

Ms Leah Brennan is a 23-year-old woman whose son, Alfie, is six weeks old. Her community midwife has referred her to the perinatal mental health team because of low mood and anxiety since the birth. She had one episode of depression at 19 and has never self-harmed or used drugs. She has told the midwife she does not want medication for now. Her partner Dean, the baby's father, has been drinking heavily and smoking a lot of cannabis; he has shouted at her and, on one occasion, shook Alfie briefly when he would not stop crying. The three of them share a small, overcrowded one-bedroom flat. Please see Ms Brennan at the clinic, take a history focused on the risks to her and to Alfie, respond to her worries, and agree with her a plan to manage those risks.

Your job: Take a history focused on the risks to Leah and to Alfie, respond to her worries, and agree with her a plan to manage those risks.
Tell yourself first

Keep it sharp

Given this task, I need to:

  1. Assess mood, anxiety, sleep, bonding and the episode at 19; screen out psychosis and mania
  2. Ask directly about suicide, self-harm and thoughts of harming Alfie
  3. Pin down the shaking: when, how long, Dean's state, Alfie since, who has checked him
  4. Screen every form of abuse: physical, sexual, financial, coercive control, isolation
  5. Tell her honestly about the same-day children's social care referral
  6. Agree a written plan: paediatric check today, Mum's tonight, IDVA, review within a week
  7. Ask what could derail it and agree a safe contact route

Structure

What you have to cover

  • 1. Opening (name, greet Alfie, midwife's referral, agree the plan)
  • 2. Her mood (low mood, anxiety, sleep, bonding, depression at 19, psychosis and mania screen)
  • 3. Risk to and from Leah (suicide, self-harm, thoughts of harming Alfie, substances)
  • 4. The shaking (when, how long, Dean's state, Alfie since, checked by anyone?)
  • 5. Dean and home (drink, cannabis, violence, sexual, money, phone, isolation, the flat)
  • 6. Her worries and supports (fear of removal, Dean's reaction, her mum)
  • 7. Safeguarding and the plan (duty to share, social care today, paediatric check, DASH, IDVA, her treatment choice)
  • 8. Barriers and close (what could derail it, safe contact, teach-back, safety net, review within a week)

Watch out

Traps to avoid

Focusing only on her mood
Greet Alfie, observe him, and ask risk in all five directions
Promising confidentiality early
Never say 'this stays between us'; explain limits honestly
No same-day check for a shaken baby
Ring the paediatric registrar; she takes him today
Threatening, or hiding, the referral
Tell her plainly, frame it as support, report her protective acts
Not asking what could stop the plan
Ask about barriers; agree contact via her mum's number
