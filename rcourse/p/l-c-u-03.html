Demanding his records in A&E: a mental state assessment

Saturday 15:10, mental health liaison · A&E majors cubicle, general hospital

The task

Marcus Lindqvist is a 34-year-old man who walked into A&E today asking to see his full medical records immediately. He is not known to mental health services. Nursing staff say he is loud, restless and behaving in an unusual way, and wonder whether he has a mental illness. They have asked the mental health liaison team to assess him. Please carry out a mental state assessment of Marcus, describing what you observe and eliciting key symptoms, then present your findings.

Your job: In about ten minutes, assess Marcus's mental state, describing what you observe and eliciting the key symptoms and risks, then present a structured summary and a safe next step to the A&E registrar.
Tell yourself first

Keep it sharp

Given this task, I need to:

  1. Seat him within 2 min by agreeing to help with the records
  2. Short questions, one at a time
  3. One concrete example each: appearance, behaviour, speech, affect
  4. Elicit the grandiose belief, why the records, persecution, voices
  5. Ask sleep, spending, driving, substances, sex, harm
  6. Present in MSE order: probable mania, bloods + drug screen, no driving
  7. Know the plan if he walks out: no 5(2) in A&E
The exact words

Your opening

YouHello, I'm Dr Chinonso, one of the psychiatry doctors. Are you Marcus Lindqvist?
MarcusThat's me, and I'm very busy, Doctor. Very, very busy.
YouThanks for seeing me. I understand you want your records; I'd like to help with that.
MarcusFinally, someone sensible! Nobody here listens.
YouCan we sit for about ten minutes first so I understand what's been going on?
MarcusTen minutes. Fine.
Structure

What you have to cover

  • 1. Opening (name, align with records request, set 10 min)
  • 2. Appearance and behaviour (clothes, pacing, eye contact, overfamiliar, distractible)
  • 3. Speech (rate, volume, pressure, hard to interrupt, flight of ideas)
  • 4. Mood and affect (subjective 0-10, elated, irritable, labile)
  • 5. Thought form and content (grandiose belief, conviction, why records, persecution)
  • 6. Perceptions and cognition (voices, messages, orientation, attention)
  • 7. Insight and risk (sleep, spending, driving, alcohol, drugs, sex, harm)
  • 8. Summary and close (bloods + UDS, records via SAR, legal plan, present to registrar)

Watch out

Traps to avoid

Arguing about the records or the app
Agree what you can; SAR form
Long multi-part questions
One short question at a time
Forgetting drugs and organic causes
Bloods, UDS, obs, neuro check
Missing driving and money
Ask how he got here; advise no driving
Planning Section 5(2) in A&E
Not lawful there; MCA if no capacity; AMHP referral
