Monday 13:45, drug and alcohol clinic · Community drug and alcohol service, outpatient clinic

The task

Mr Liam O'Connor, a 32-year-old man, was sent by his GP to the drug and alcohol outpatient clinic because he has been using several different drugs over some years. He is out of work and lives in a rented flat with his girlfriend, who is pregnant. Please take a history of his drug use and establish whether he meets the features of a dependence syndrome.

Your job: Take a history of his drug use: each drug, amount, route and cost, the story from first use, the features of a dependence syndrome, complications, past treatment, background and risk, including to the unborn baby. Summarise and outline what help is available.
Tell yourself first

Keep it sharp

Given this task, I need to:

  1. State confidentiality and its limits, including the unborn baby, in minute one
  2. Drug by drug: amount, frequency, cost, route, sharing, last use
  3. Story from first use: treatment, abstinence, relapse, prison
  4. Each dependence feature with his own example
  5. Complications: overdose, abscess, BBV testing, mood, debt, crime
  6. Risk: mixing, using alone, suicide, safeguarding the baby
  7. Name opioid dependence, outline treatment, hand over naloxone
The exact words

Your opening

YouHello, I'm Dr Chinonso, one of the psychiatry doctors. Are you Liam O'Connor?
LiamYeah.
YouThanks for coming, Liam. Your GP mentioned you've been using a few different drugs.
LiamMainly gear, a bit of other stuff.
YouI won't judge. I just want to understand, so we can work out what help fits. What you tell me stays with our team and your GP, unless someone, including a child or unborn baby, is at serious risk. Is that OK?
LiamYeah. Fine.
Structure

What you have to cover

  • 1. Opening (name, no judgement, confidentiality and its limits)
  • 2. Drugs used (heroin, blues, crack, alcohol; amount, cost, route, sharing, last use)
  • 3. The story and past treatment (first use, injecting, methadone, abstinence, prison, relapse)
  • 4. Dependence features (craving, control, tolerance, withdrawal, salience, persistence despite harm)
  • 5. Complications (overdose, abscess, BBV, mood, money, police)
  • 6. Background (Chloe, pregnancy, housing, work)
  • 7. Risk (mixing, using alone, suicide, drugs at home, safeguarding)
  • 8. Summary and close (dependence named, treatment, naloxone, urine test, follow-up)

Watch out

Traps to avoid

Asking only about heroin
Ask 'anything else?': benzos, crack, alcohol, cannabis
Reading the criteria as a checklist
Get each feature as his own example
Asking only about needles
Spoons, filters and water too
Avoiding the baby, or using it as a threat
Raise it early as shared concern; involve midwife together
No naloxone
Hand over the kit and show him and Chloe
Finish

Close it well

YouCan you tell me what we've agreed? I'll see you next week, and if anyone's unresponsive, naloxone and 999.
LiamUrine test, naloxone, don't use alone, come back next week to start a script. And you'll talk to the midwife with me.
