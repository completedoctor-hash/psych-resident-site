Teenage overdose after bullying: history and risk before discharge

Saturday 10:15, on call for the general hospital · Children's ward, side room

The task

Miss Chloe Nwosu is a 15-year-old girl admitted to the children's ward from A&E after taking about 80 paracetamol tablets. She needed an acetylcysteine infusion but has now been declared medically fit by the paediatric team. Her mother is waiting in the parents' room. You are the psychiatry doctor covering the hospital. Please take a history about the overdose and the factors that led to it, and assess her current risk.

Your job: In about ten minutes, take a focused history of the overdose and what led to it, and assess Chloe's current risk, so the team can decide on a safe discharge plan.
Tell yourself first

Keep it sharp

Given this task, I need to:

  1. State confidentiality and its limits before any question about the overdose
  2. Before, during, after: source, planning, setting, note, expectation, help-seeking
  3. Find at least three causes: bullying, its online spread, low mood, little support
  4. Ask if any images were sexual, and if anyone at home hurts her
  5. Ask thoughts now and what would make them stronger
  6. Agree a named plan: medicines locked away, safety plan, school lead, CAMHS within 48 h
The exact words

Your opening

YouHello, I'm Dr Chinonso, one of the psychiatry doctors. Are you Chloe?
ChloeYeah.
YouThanks for talking to me, Chloe. I'm glad you're physically better. I'd like to understand what happened. It'll take about ten minutes.
ChloeOkay.
YouOur chat is kept private, except if I'm worried you or someone else isn't safe. Then I'd have to share it, and I'd tell you first.
ChloeFine.
Structure

What you have to cover

  • 1. Opening (name, glad she's well, confidentiality and its limits)
  • 2. The overdose (source, planning, setting, note, alone, expectation)
  • 3. After the overdose (help sought, relief or regret, wish to die now)
  • 4. Why now (bullying, group chat, escalation, who knows)
  • 5. Mood and mental health (low mood, sleep, eating, self-harm, substances)
  • 6. Safeguarding (sexual images, abuse, home safety)
  • 7. Current risk (thoughts now, what makes them stronger, what she'd do)
  • 8. Protective factors (friend, art, Mum, safety plan)
  • 9. Summary and close (summary, Mum, school lead, CAMHS, medicines)

Watch out

Traps to avoid

Mum in the room throughout
See Chloe alone first; then Mum
Judging intent from her calm today
Judge by planning, setting, number and expectation
Not asking whether images were sexual
Ask once, plainly; refer the same day if yes
Risk score to justify discharge
Formulation in words; NICE NG225
Forgetting means at home
All medicines locked away before she goes home
Finish

Close it well

YouSo, months of bullying that followed you home, low mood, then a planned overdose that you're relieved you survived. Have I got that right?
ChloeYeah. That's it.
YouWhat would you like me to tell Mum, and what would you rather tell her yourself?
ChloeYou can tell her about the bullying. Not the photos yet.
YouThank you, Chloe. That took courage. I'll speak to Mum now, then come back and we'll do the plan together.
