Starting clozapine at home

Monday 10:00, CMHT clinic · Community mental health team base, clinic room 2

The task

Ms Aisha Rahman is a 32-year-old woman with schizophrenia that has not responded to two antipsychotics. She takes olanzapine 20 mg at night, yet she still hears derogatory voices and believes neighbours are plotting against her. At her last clinic review the team suggested clozapine, started in the community with daily input from the Home Treatment Team. She said no at the time, but has now told her care coordinator she is ready to give it a go and has come to the CMHT today. Please speak to her, explain what clozapine is, how it would be started at home and the side effects she needs to know about, and answer her questions.

Your job: In about ten minutes, check how she is now, explain why clozapine, how it starts at home, the blood tests and the side effects she must act on, and answer her questions.
Tell yourself first

Keep it sharp

Given this task, I need to:

  1. Check voices, beliefs, olanzapine adherence, self-harm thoughts
  2. Ask what she already knows and fears before explaining
  3. Explain treatment resistance and the home start: 12.5 mg, slow rise, daily HTT
  4. Give the blood schedule: weekly 18 wks, 2-weekly to 1 yr, then monthly
  5. Name 4 common side effects with fixes and 3 same-day red flags
  6. Cover the 48-hour missed-dose rule and smoking
  7. Teach-back of red flags; give HTT and crisis numbers

Structure

What you have to cover

  • 1. Opening (name, praise her decision, agree the plan)

  • 3. Why clozapine (treatment resistance, chance of improvement, time to work)
  • 4. Before starting (baseline bloods, ECG, weight, smoking, monitoring service)
  • 5. Starting at home (12.5 mg, slow rise, daily HTT, olanzapine cross-taper)

  • 8. Practical rules (48 hours, smoking, driving, pregnancy, consent)

Why clozapine

Two adequate trials failed = treatment-resistant; about half improve; months to full effect

Two different antipsychotics at good doses haven't settled things. That's called treatment-resistant schizophrenia.
Around half of people improve, sometimes a lot. It can take a few months to show its full effect.
So it's not just another tablet like the others?
Why can't I just go up on the olanzapine?

Law and safety net

Before starting: tests and registration

FBC, U&E, LFT, glucose or HbA1c, lipids, troponin, CRP, ECG, weight, BP, smoking; register with monitoring service

First we'd check bloods, including your white cells, sugar, cholesterol and heart markers, plus an ECG, your weight and blood pressure.
We register you with a clozapine monitoring service. The pharmacy can only give it out with a safe blood result.
Do you smoke? How many a day?
About fifteen a day.

Starting at home

12.5 mg first dose, slow rise over 2-3 wks, daily HTT obs for 2 wks, olanzapine tapered

You'd start at home with a tiny dose, twelve and a half milligrams, and go up slowly over two to three weeks.
The Home Treatment Team comes every day for the first two weeks and takes your pulse, temperature and blood pressure.
The olanzapine comes down gradually as clozapine goes up.
Do I have to come into hospital?
Okay. That's less scary.

Blood tests and the infection rule

Neutropenia ~3 in 100, severe <1 in 100; weekly 18 wks, 2-weekly to 1 yr, monthly; sore throat or fever = ring today

Clozapine lowers white cells in about three in a hundred people, and seriously in under one in a hundred.
Bloods are weekly for the first eighteen weeks, then every two weeks until a year, then monthly.
A sore throat, temperature or flu-like feeling means ring us that day for a blood test.
Weekly? I hate needles.
Sore throat, ring you. Okay.

Common side effects and their fixes

Sedation, night drooling (hyoscine, off-licence), fast pulse, postural dizziness, weight gain

Sleepiness is common, so we give the bigger dose at bedtime.
Drooling at night is common, and a tablet can help.
Will you tell me if you feel dizzy when you stand up?
Will I put on even more weight? I've gained loads on olanzapine.

Constipation can kill

Laxative from day 1; no bowel opening for 3 days = ring; MHRA warning

Constipation is common and can become dangerous, so you'll take a laxative from day one.

Heart and fits

Myocarditis mostly first 2 months: chest pain, breathlessness, fever = same day or 999; seizures at higher levels

Rarely, it can inflame the heart, mostly in the first two months.
Chest pain, breathlessness, or feeling feverish and unwell: ring us that day, or 999 if it's severe.
At higher doses it can cause fits, so we go slowly and check levels.

Smoking and missed doses

Smoking speeds clearance; stopping raises levels, check a level; missed >48 h = re-titrate from low dose

Please don't change your smoking without telling us.
If you miss more than two days of doses, don't just restart. Ring us.
That includes switching to vapes, or going into a smoke-free hospital.
