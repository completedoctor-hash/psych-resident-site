Depression not lifting after two drugs: the next step

Monday 14:30, community mental health clinic · Outpatient clinic room

The task

Mr Jeremy Pearce is a 47-year-old man referred to the community mental health clinic. He has had three previous episodes of depression, each of which resolved on fluoxetine 20 mg. About six months ago he relapsed with low mood, low energy, loss of enjoyment, poor concentration and poor appetite. His GP restarted fluoxetine 20 mg, raised it to 40 mg after six weeks without benefit, then after another six weeks with no response switched him to venlafaxine 150 mg. His mood has improved a little, but his other symptoms remain. The diagnosis has been confirmed. Please speak to Mr Pearce, take his circumstances into account, and explain the treatment options available to him.

Your job: In about ten minutes, check why he is not better (adherence, side effects, thyroid, alcohol, physical health, stress), ask about suicide, then recommend one clear next step with its monitoring, add talking therapy, and say what follows if it fails.
Tell yourself first

Keep it sharp

Given this task, I need to:

  1. Ask what is better and what is not
  2. Check adherence, side effects, alcohol and stress
  3. Ask 3+ hypothyroid symptoms and arrange TFTs, U&E, FBC
  4. Ask directly about suicidal thoughts
  5. Recommend venlafaxine 225 mg, BP today and at 2 weeks, never stop suddenly
  6. Offer CBT alongside, and name augmentation as plan B, ECT later
  7. Teach-back, review within 4 weeks, crisis route

JerryYes. Jerry.

Structure

What you have to cover

  • 1. Opening (name, GP letter, agree plan)
  • 2. Where he is now (mood, energy, enjoyment, sleep, appetite, concentration)
  • 3. Why not better (adherence, side effects, thyroid, BP and heart, alcohol, stress)
  • 4. Risk (suicidal thoughts, plans, protective factors)
  • 5. His circumstances (work, weight worry, past therapy, preferences)
  • 6. Medication: one step (bloods first; venlafaxine 225 mg; BP; never stop suddenly)
  • 7. Therapy (CBT 16-20 sessions, early or video, occupational health)
  • 8. If still stuck (augment: aripiprazole or lithium; avoid mirtazapine; ECT, rTMS later)
  • 9. Summary and close (teach-back, review 2-4 weeks, crisis line)

One recommendation, with its monitoring

If TFTs normal: venlafaxine 150 to 225 mg; BP today and at 2 weeks; side effects; never stop suddenly

If your thyroid is normal, my recommendation is to increase the venlafaxine to 225 mg.
You've already had some benefit, so building on it usually works better than switching.
I'll check your blood pressure today and again in two weeks. Please never stop it suddenly.
Why not just switch to something new?
Will a higher dose mean more side effects?
Makes sense. So not a new tablet?

Get it done

Pair it with talking therapy

CBT 16-20 sessions; early or video; occupational health with consent

Tablets work best alongside talking therapy. I'd like to refer you for CBT, about 16 to 20 sessions.
Sessions can be early or by video, and occupational health can help with a flexible start if you agree.
I can't take time off for therapy. I'll lose my job.
Early mornings I could do.

Plan B, and ECT in its place

After 4-6 weeks: add aripiprazole or lithium; avoid mirtazapine (weight); ECT, rTMS, specialist service much later

If there's not enough change after four to six weeks, the next step is adding a second medicine, like aripiprazole or lithium.
I'd avoid mirtazapine first, because of your weight worry.
ECT works well, but it's for severe depression when other steps haven't worked. We're nowhere near that for you.
My mate had electric shock treatment. Is that next for me?
Good. That's a relief.

I'll try the higher dose, if the blood test's normal.

Watch out

Traps to avoid

Listing every treatment with no recommendation
One step, with dose and monitoring, then the fallback
Jumping to ECT or a third switch despite partial response
Build on venlafaxine first
Missing the thyroid symptoms
Ask cold, constipation, dry skin, weight; test today
Raising venlafaxine with no BP check or stopping warning
BP today and at 2 weeks; never stop suddenly
Ignoring his weight worry
Avoid mirtazapine as the first add-on
Finish
