Periods stopped on risperidone: high prolactin and a shared plan

Tuesday 14:20 · Outpatient clinic room, community mental health team base

The task

Natalia Kowalczyk, a 37-year-old woman with paranoid schizophrenia, has been well in the community for 12 months since leaving hospital, with no current psychotic symptoms. She takes risperidone 6 mg daily without missing doses. Olanzapine had been stopped earlier because of side effects. At her last clinic visit she mentioned problems with her periods over several months, so bloods were sent. Her prolactin is 1100 mIU/L (normal 100-410); before risperidone it was normal. Please take a brief history of her symptoms and discuss a management plan with her in the outpatient clinic.

Your job: Take a focused history of the effects of high prolactin, exclude pregnancy, other drugs and a pituitary cause, explain the result in plain words, and agree a management plan with her.
Tell yourself first

Keep it sharp

Given this task, I need to:

  1. Find out what she knows, then ask about periods, breasts, milk, libido, skin
  2. Exclude pregnancy (urine test today), other drugs, thyroid and kidney causes
  3. Ask about headache and visual change: pituitary red flags
  4. Explain prolactin, her level and the risperidone link in plain words
  5. Explain why it's worth treating: bones, fertility, possible breast link
  6. Offer four options and let her choose; no dopamine agonist yourself
  7. Plan contraception, perinatal referral, repeat bloods, review and GP letter
The exact words

Your opening

YouHello, I'm Dr Chinonso, one of the psychiatry doctors. Are you Natalia Kowalczyk?
NataliaYes, Natalia.
YouThanks for coming back, Natalia. Your blood results are in, and I'd like to go through them with you.
NataliaOkay. Is it bad?
YouNothing dangerous, and we can sort it. First, could you tell me more about your periods?
NataliaThey got lighter and shorter from about the summer. Then last month nothing at all.
Structure

What you have to cover

  • 1. Opening (name, results back, reassure, what she knows)
  • 2. Period changes (lighter, stopped, how long, regular before)
  • 3. Other prolactin effects (breast soreness, milk, libido, acne, hair)
  • 4. Other causes (pregnancy, drugs, thyroid, kidney, pituitary red flags)
  • 5. Mental state now (well, voices, adherence, what keeps her well)
  • 6. Explain and long-term risks (prolactin, her level, risperidone link, bones, fertility, breast)
  • 7. Options (switch, add aripiprazole, lower dose, monitor)
  • 8. Pregnancy plan and tests (contraception, perinatal team, folic acid, repeat bloods)
  • 9. Summary and close (teach-back, safety net, GP letter, review in 2 weeks)

Explain the result, the cause and the risks

Hormone from a gland under the brain; 1,100 vs normal before; dopamine brake; bones, fertility, breast

Prolactin is a hormone from a small gland under the brain. When it's high, it switches periods off and lowers sex drive.
Risperidone blocks dopamine, which is the brake on prolactin. That's the most likely cause.
Over years, high prolactin can thin the bones and make pregnancy harder, so it's worth treating.
So it's the tablets, not a tumour?
Why didn't anyone warn me about this?
I
Get it done

Four options, and she chooses

Switch to aripiprazole; add aripiprazole 5 mg; lower dose; monitor. No dopamine agonist yourself

One: swap gradually to aripiprazole, which hardly raises prolactin and rarely causes weight gain.
Two: stay on risperidone and add a low dose of aripiprazole, 5 mg in the morning.
Three: lower the risperidone, with some risk of the voices returning. Four: change nothing and keep checking.
Keeping you well matters most. Which feels right to you?
Adding the small one feels safer. Does it have side effects?
I'm not putting on weight again like on olanzapine.

Get it done

Pregnancy, tests and follow-up

Fertility returns fast: condoms; perinatal referral; folic acid 400 mcg; repeat prolactin, TFTs, U&Es; recheck at 3 months; GP letter

As prolactin falls, you can become fertile again quickly, so use condoms until you've decided you're ready.
Before trying, I'll refer you to our perinatal mental health team. Start folic acid, 400 micrograms a day, from when you start trying.
Today: the pregnancy test, a repeat prolactin after you've rested, thyroid and kidney tests.
Oh. I didn't know that.
Okay. That's a lot of blood.

Law and safety net

Informed choice and a clear record

Montgomery: material risks of each option incl. staying put; capacity presumed; GMC consent; document

Whichever option you choose, I'll explain what could go well and what could go wrong.
If you'd rather keep things as they are, that's your choice, and we'd keep checking your bloods.
So I don't have to change anything if I don't want to?
Watch out

Traps to avoid

Blaming the drug before excluding pregnancy
Ask, and do a urine pregnancy test today
Stopping risperidone abruptly
She is well; change gradually or add aripiprazole
Starting a dopamine agonist yourself
Can worsen psychosis; endocrinology decision
Forgetting fertility returns
Advise contraception; perinatal preconception referral
Rushing past embarrassment
Ask about sex plainly, then normalise it
