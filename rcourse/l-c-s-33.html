Monday 15:00, ward round follow-up · Older adult inpatient ward, side room

The task

Miss Edna Morrison, a 75-year-old retired seamstress, is an inpatient on the older adult ward after struggling to cope at home with worsening memory. She has recently been diagnosed with vascular dementia and had a CT head scan, the result of which she has not yet been told. She is cooperative and keen to help. Please carry out a bedside physical assessment of her parietal lobe function, explaining each step to her as you go.

Your job: Carry out a bedside parietal lobe assessment (neglect, apraxia, agnosia, Gerstmann features, constructional and sensory tests) and say what the findings mean.
Tell yourself first

Keep it sharp

Given this task, I need to:

  1. Confirm handedness, glasses, hearing and limb strength before any test
  2. Test visual fields in four quadrants, then visual inattention
  3. Test all four Gerstmann features: fingers, left-right, sums, writing
  4. Test ideomotor, ideational, dressing and constructional praxis
  5. Test stereognosis, graphaesthesia, two-point and sensory inattention, eyes closed
  6. Ask about insight, getting lost and faces
  7. Localise to left, right or both and turn it into a care plan
The exact words

Your opening

YouHello, I'm Dr Chinonso, one of the psychiatry doctors. Is it Miss Morrison?
EdnaYes, love. Edna.
YouLovely to meet you, Edna. I'd like to do some short tasks to see how parts of your brain are working.
EdnaAlright.
YouSome involve touching your hands. Is that okay? Are you right- or left-handed?
EdnaRight.
Structure

What you have to cover

  • 1. Opening (name, hand hygiene, consent, handedness, glasses, hearing, strength)
  • 2. Fields and inattention (four quadrants each eye, both hands moving at once)
  • 3. Gerstmann features (finger naming, crossed left-right, serial 7s, writing a sentence)
  • 4. Praxis (wave, mime toothbrush, letter-envelope sequence, cardigan)
  • 5. Constructional (overlapping pentagons, cube, clock at ten past five)
  • 6. Cortical sensation (coin and key, number on palm, two-point, joint position, both hands)
  • 7. Awareness and recognition (insight into deficit, getting lost, faces)
  • 8. Close and localise (left, right or both; scan question; nurse, OT, notes)

Now the other eye.

Gerstmann: sums and writing

Serial 7s or a written sum; write a sentence; handle the tears

What's one hundred take away seven? Now keep taking seven away.
Could you write me a sentence about the weather on this card?
Ninety-three... eighty... no. I can't. I measured and added up all my working life.
(Writes 'It is' and stops.) The words won't come out of the pen.

Praxis: mime and sequence

Wave, salute, brush teeth, use a key; copy hand postures; fold, insert, seal

Show me how you'd wave goodbye, then how you'd brush your teeth.
Pretend you're holding the toothbrush, rather than using your finger.
Here's a letter and an envelope. Fold it, put it in and seal it, please.
(Waves; rubs her finger across her teeth.)
(Folds, inserts and seals, slowly but in the right order.)

Drawing: pentagons, cube, clock

Overlapping pentagons, a cube, a clock with numbers and hands; look for missing left-sided detail

Law and safety net

Consent, best interests and capacity

Is it alright if I go on to the next one?

Law and safety net

The scan question

She has a right to know her CT result; don't dodge or guess; name who will tell her and when

Waiting for that is frustrating, I know. Dr Shah will go through the scan with you tomorrow, and I'll tell her today that you're asking.
Today is just some short tests. Is that okay?
Are you the one with my scan results? Nobody tells me anything.
Watch out

Traps to avoid

Half the time on the clock
Two minutes for drawing, then move on: six domains need covering.
Calling neglect without checking fields
Four quadrants each eye first, then both hands together.
Calling apraxia with a weak arm or a misunderstood instruction
Check strength and a simple command first; give one clearer second try.
Telling her scan results you haven't read
Name who will tell her and when, and pass the message on today.
