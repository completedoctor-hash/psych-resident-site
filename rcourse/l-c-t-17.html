Found wandering at night: the granddaughter's account

Thursday 21:40 · Acute medical unit, on call

The task

Mr Harold Brennan, a 79-year-old retired mechanic, was brought to hospital this evening after the police found him walking around his estate in his pyjamas. No other doctor has assessed him yet. His granddaughter, Leah Brennan, has come in because her mother is at work. Harold lives alone in a small flat with no carers. Please speak to Leah, take a collateral history and assess the risks to him.

Your job: Take a collateral history from Leah about tonight and the months before, screen for delirium and physical causes, and assess the risks to Harold so that a safe decision can be made about tonight.
Tell yourself first

Keep it sharp

Given this task, I need to:

  1. Tell Leah he is safe, then agree a quick pace of short questions
  2. Get tonight before, during and after: door, clothes, time, injuries, first time?
  3. Ask 'gradual or sudden?' and get two concrete examples
  4. Screen for delirium: illness, tablets, falls, head injury, alcohol, bowels
  5. Check function: meals, bills, tablets, money, driving
  6. Risk in three parts: physical, social, psychiatric, including gas, exploitation and grief
  7. Hold Leah's distress and give a clear plan: tests tonight, not home alone
The exact words

Structure

What you have to cover

  • 1. Opening (name, he is safe, consent, set the pace)
  • 2. Tonight in detail (before, during, after, door, clothing, time out, injuries)
  • 3. Recent months (self-care, gas hob, memory, gradual or stepwise)
  • 4. Delirium and physical (illness, new tablets, falls, head injury, stroke signs, alcohol, bowels)
  • 5. Function and mood (meals, bills, tablets, driving, grief)
  • 6. Risk (gas, wandering, cold, falls, exploitation, self-neglect, suicide)
  • 7. Her feelings (grandmother's death, fear of decline)
  • 8. Summary and close (tests tonight, delirium before dementia, not home alone, support)

Watch out

Traps to avoid

Calling it dementia tonight
Delirium first: tests tonight, memory checks once he is well
Jumping to memory questions
Get tonight before, during and after first
Forgetting gas, car and money
Ask about each by name in the risk section
Missing grief
Ask about mood, sleep and appetite since his wife died
Leaving Leah without a plan
Ask how she is, name her loss, give the plan and the ward number
