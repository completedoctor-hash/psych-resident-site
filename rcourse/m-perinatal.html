Before and during pregnancy
LessonWhat you will be able to doLength
1'Will my baby get schizophrenia?' Genetic risk at 12 weeks
Explain the causes of schizophrenia and the risk to her baby in clear numbers, support her decision about the pregnancy without steering it, and plan her perinatal care.		15 min
2Trying for a baby on lithium: risks, options and a perinatal plan
Explain the risks of lithium and of relapse in pregnancy and after the birth, set out the options with their trade-offs, explain monitoring, fertility and breastfeeding, and agree a written perinatal plan she owns.		15 min
3Valproate and wanting a baby: the risks, the rules, a planned switch
Explain the risks of valproate in pregnancy and the MHRA safety rules, address her worries about relapse, sertraline and social services, and plan a safe, supervised switch before she stops contraception.		15 min
415 weeks pregnant on quetiapine: the gestational diabetes question
Review quetiapine in this pregnancy, explain its risks including gestational diabetes and breastfeeding, compare the alternatives, explain her postpartum relapse risk, give a recommendation, and agree a plan with obstetrics for early screening and support.		14 min
After the birth
LessonWhat you will be able to doLength
5Five months postnatal and not improving: what's keeping her stuck
With the health visitor, summarise the course so far, explore the factors keeping the depression going, check risk, and recommend specific biological, psychological and social next steps, with a plan B if they fail.		14 min
6Washing the bottles six times: a postnatal OCD history
Take a history of how her obsessions and compulsions have changed since the birth, link them to the birth and her past OCD, assess their impact on her functioning and on the baby, screen for other illness, and assess risk.		15 min
