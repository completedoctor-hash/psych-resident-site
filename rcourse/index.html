How every lesson runs
  1. The task, read to you in full.
  2. Keep it sharp: what to tell yourself before you start.
  3. Your opening: the exact first words, and what you will hear back.
  4. What you have to cover: the structure, as broad headings.
  5. Practicalities A to Z: most of the lesson. The exact questions for each point, what the patient or relative is likely to say, and how that takes you to the next point.
  6. Traps to avoid, and what to do instead.
  7. Close it well: the summary, the safety net and the note you write.

Then open the challenge itself and practise it, with the full practice run.
