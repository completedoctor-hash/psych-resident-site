Refund policy

Fair and simple. Last updated 6 November 2026.

14-day refund

If you are not happy, email completedoctor@gmail.com within 14 days of buying a course and we will refund you in full, as long as you have not worked through a substantial part of it (more than about a quarter of the chapters or questions).

Digital content and your right to cancel

Because access starts straight away, by buying you ask us to begin supplying the course immediately. Under UK consumer law this means the statutory 14-day cancellation right ends once you start using the course; the refund promise above is offered in addition to your legal rights.

Technical problems

If you paid but cannot get into your course, contact us first: we will fix your access or refund you.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the card you paid with, usually within 5 to 10 working days. Course access ends when the refund is made.
