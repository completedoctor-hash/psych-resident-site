The 2027 syllabus  ·  in force from January 2027

The whole curriculum. Every line of it.

This is the Royal College of Psychiatrists' new syllabus, the one every paper from January 2027 is set from, taken verbatim from the College's own Paper A and Paper B syllabus documents: 961 numbered items across thirteen domains, four in Paper A and nine in Paper B, in the College's numbering, its headings and its exact wording. Nothing is paraphrased, summarised, renumbered or added. Sitting in November 2026? The 2021 syllabus is still here. Nothing is summarised away and nothing is left out. Each line opens into the teaching: the plain-English version first, then the numbered points, the flashcards, the notebook, and the book on the College reading list where you can check it.
