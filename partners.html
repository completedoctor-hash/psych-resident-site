Partnership invitation · Medical schools in low- and middle-income countries

Psychiatry teaching for every medical student, wherever they train.

The Psychiatric Resident invites universities and medical schools in low- and middle-income countries to partner with us. Together we can give your students tailored, high-quality psychiatry teaching at a much reduced institutional cost.

Start a conversation →
Why this matters

Mental illness is common everywhere. Psychiatry teaching is not.

In many low- and middle-income countries, medical schools have very few psychiatrists to teach, depend on visiting lecturers, and give psychiatry only a small share of the curriculum. Students may qualify with little confidence in recognising and managing mental illness, and few choose psychiatry as a career. Yet these graduates are often the first, and sometimes only, doctors a person with a mental illness will see.

We have built a complete, structured psychiatry teaching portal. We would like to put it to work where it can do the most good.

What we offer partners
01Tailored modules

Undergraduate psychiatry modules mapped to your own curriculum, rotation length and assessment format.

02A ready teaching portal

Structured chapters, clinical vignettes, diagrams, flashcards and exam-style questions, available on any phone or computer.

03Much reduced pricing

Institutional access for whole student cohorts at a fraction of individual prices, with fee waivers considered case by case.

04Support for your faculty

Teaching outlines, case discussions and question banks that local lecturers can use in class or clinic.

05Assessment and progress

Formative quizzes and progress summaries so you can see how your cohort is doing.

06A route into psychiatry

A pathway from undergraduate teaching to postgraduate exam preparation for students who choose psychiatry.

How it works
